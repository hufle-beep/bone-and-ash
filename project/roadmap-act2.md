James's presence caused a slight regeneration; the central databank is reachable again and reached out to his omnitool. **The ship will never be truly alive again, nor regenerate enough to fly.** There he gets the sensor data and **realises his world is the outlier, and this world and the others the norm** (the plot point of v0.11.165, LO1, below).
- **The garden:** James establishes a garden around the growing Hearth Tree seedling (planted at Ashfall in Act 1's return) and cares for both: mostly food plants and medicinal herbs; he teaches the locals agriculture. In his culture biology, engineering, agriculture, horticulture and gardening are mostly the same thing, because of the nature of their technology, and he has a doctorate in biology.
