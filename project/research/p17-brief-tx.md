# Run TX (owner pick P17-A, TX: ten tic swaps)

You reword single sentences in a hand-written text RPG. Read ONLY: this brief, `/tmp/p17/common.md` (tone and rules: they bind you) and `/tmp/p17/tx-sents.md` (for each text, the phrase repeated three times in it and the sentences that hold it). Do not read other repo files.

For each of the ten tics, rewrite **one** of its sentences (the one where the repeat is least the joke) so the phrase occurs one time fewer in that text, changing as few words as you can (ideally 2–6) and keeping the sentence's meaning, rhythm and tone. Leave the deliberate echo where the repetition is the joke (e.g. Old Wen's "A ladder is only a ladder…", "forty years of research" / "forty years of me sitting on the research", the fence posts): reword a different sentence of that tic instead. Don't introduce a new repeat with the text's other sentences. In `breakthrough`, do not touch "The ground, it seems, has let go of you."

## Output
Write `/tmp/p17/drafts-tx.txt`, one line per tic: `tx:<scene>:<phrase> | <the old sentence exactly as in the file> => <the new sentence>`; then `# notes` as in common.md.
