supper_roof:2:4 | Tonight Mei Lin bets you that the widow's lamp will go out before the dyer's. ¶ "Stakes?" ¶ "The loser carries my mother's water in the morning. Both buckets." She settles on her elbows to watch the lane. "The widow sleeps early. She likes to be up before anyone else's faults are." ¶ "And the dyer?" ¶ "Owes the miller. He sits up doing sums until they come out differently." ¶ You back the dyer's lamp to go first, on principle. Both burn on. A shutter creaks, a basin is emptied into the lane, and the widow's window goes dark. ¶ "You've watched this lane before." ¶ "Every night since I could climb." She says it into her sleeve, shaking. "It was never a bet. It was a lesson."
supper_roof:2:4:r | The dyer's lamp is still burning when the thatch goes cold, and his sums are no nearer. On the ladder she tells you where the yoke hangs and which bucket leaks, as a kindness to the defeated. You climb to the herb loft and sleep like a man with water to carry.
supper_roof:2:6 | Mei Lin's mother has three rules about the lodger, and Mei Lin recites them to you from under the blanket she brought up the ladder. ¶ "One. He is not to be fed between meals; it encourages him." She passes you a roasted chestnut out of her sleeve. ¶ "Two. He is not to be asked about his country. The answers keep a household awake." ¶ "What do you want to know?" ¶ "Whether it has roofs a person may sit on." ¶ "Several." ¶ "Good." She shakes out the blanket until it covers your shoulder as well as hers, and looks hard at the lane while she does it. "Three concerns distance." ¶ "How much distance?" ¶ "She gave no figure. I have always thought that careless of her."
supper_roof:2:6:r | The chestnuts run out before the rules do; there is a fourth, about the ladder after dark, and you break it together once the thatch is cold. At the bottom she folds the blanket with great correctness, as evidence. You go up to the herb loft smelling of chestnut smoke, and sleep.
# notes
- 2:4: a bet on the lane's lamps (the widow and the dyer are the first week's neighbours; the miller is new, named once, cut "Owes the miller." if no miller exists). No wine, no tribute; the loser's forfeit is tomorrow's water, no fixed day.
- 2:6: her mother's rules, quoted and broken; the touch is one shared blanket over a shoulder, at the old texts' level. "the lodger" is the mother's word for James. No tribute named.
- Neither uses Yuki, so no form keys. 2:5 and its result untouched.
