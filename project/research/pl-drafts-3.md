# PL drafts, part 3 (v0.11.189, RV1; Fable, run 3, about 70k tokens; word for word, then Claude's one cut)

### PA
"Which rung?" Mei Lin asks. "Core Formation," you say. She sits down on her pack, hard. "The Sect's elders are Core Formation. They spent centuries on it. They get carried places." Ya eyes the clearing sky. "Can he beat an elder?" "Ask an elder," you say.

### MA
Mei Lin prods your shoulder with one finger, checking it for heat. "Well? What are you?" "Core Formation," you say. She takes the finger back. "We kneel to those. I have knelt to one, holding a basket. And Ya chose this to sulk through." "I can describe it," you say. "It is mostly sitting."

### TA
Mei Lin's head comes up the crack, braced for a corpse. She finds you sitting under a clear sky. "The storm was over this tree," she says. "I ran." Ya climbs past her and sniffs. "No burnt meat. Which rung, scholar?" "Core Formation." Mei Lin uses a word her mother never taught her.

### PC
"What is it now?" Mei Lin asks you. You tell her. She laughs until the sky has finished leaving. "Sect masters have words like that. It costs them lifetimes, and there are banners." Ya grins. "And they float up like old smoke. You'll go up like a thrown rock, scholar." "Thank you," you say.

### MC
"Which one?" Mei Lin asks, and you tell her. She circles you, as she would a herb she can't name. "That is among the sect masters. I would not be let within a field of one. And Ya took her spear and her temper and missed it. I am going to enjoy telling her."

### TC
Ya arrives first, spear in her teeth, and takes it out. "How high, scholar?" You tell her. "Sect masters do that on a peak, with a crowd. You did it in a dead tree." Mei Lin starts laughing, then looks at the sky for some while without any. "It had a vacancy," you say.

### TD
Mei Lin climbs up, finds you whole, and stares. "Name?" You give it. "That is a realm from the old stories," she says, not quite steadily. "In those the man stops answering when he is called." Ya's head appears at her elbow. "He'll answer," Ya says. "He owes me a hunt."

### PF
Mei Lin has kept her arms folded through whatever the sky did. "What is this one?" You tell her. "Immortal," she says, trying it on you. "I should bow. I can't. I watched you apologise to a mushroom." Ya plants her spear. "Fight me anyway, scholar. I want to say I lost to one."

### MF
Mei Lin lets out a breath she has kept through all of it. "Tell me." You do. "Immortal," she says. "There should be a mountain under you, and a thousand people on their faces." She looks round the camp. "There's me." She is smiling. "And Ya will come back wanting to fight one."

### TF
Ya comes up the crack with her spear ready, sees nothing to stab, and takes it personally. Mei Lin sits across from you. "Which is this?" You say it. "Immortal," she repeats. "In a hollow tree, in that coat." She isn't laughing. "Should we kneel?" "Please don't," you say. "I'd have to get up."

Claude's cut: TC "Ya arrives first" → "Ya climbs up first" (TG2: the Old Tree's texts climb up; 55 words).
