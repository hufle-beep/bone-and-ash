## PL — the women at realm 2 and up (the `breakthrough` scene)

**Background.** Each time James breaks through, Heaven's tribulation comes, looks, finds nothing to burn and leaves (realms 10–11: it does not come at all). At his first (Foundation) with Mei Lin and Ya travelling with him, they react (`now.md`, "Foundation"). At every later realm the scene ends on Yuki alone (`now.md`, "The realms' last lines"), even when both women are right there. Your lines go **after** that Yuki line and end the scene. Measured: 69–92% of later breakthroughs in the bot games happen with the women there; a player who sits a lot sees 3–5 of these in a game, so each step needs its own text.

**Where (three places):**
- **P, the party:** both women with him at a camp or on the road (the Blackroot's camps, the Return's camp). They saw it from a few steps off.
- **M, Mei Lin alone:** at the Ashen Hills camp while Ya has walked off offended (she took her spear). Mei Lin may mention Ya; Ya does not appear.
- **T, the Old Tree:** he sat up inside the great dead tree; the women were below and come up the crack after it (as the Foundation's "tree" text). They find him unburnt, the sky clear (or, at realms 10–11, nothing to find).

**The steps (one text per step and place: 18 texts):**
- **A** realm 2, Core Formation (the second time they have a tribulation near them; it behaves as at Foundation).
- **B** realm 3, True Core.
- **C** realms 4–5, Golden Core and Origin Core (Golden Core is where this world's people at last leave the ground, slowly and with ceremony; he has been leaving it since Foundation).
- **D** realms 6–9, Soul Embryo to Immortal Ascension (names out of legend; the sky still comes and still leaves).
- **E** realms 10–11, Mahayana and Earth Immortal: **the sky does not come at all.** He waits; nothing.
- **F** realms 12–14, True Immortal to Heavenly Immortal (the sky comes in full and still finds nothing; the last rung bears Heaven's own name).

**Constraints:**
- 20–55 words each; 1–3 sentences, or a short exchange; a deadpan James line to close in about half of them, not all.
- **Each text must read right if it is the first breakthrough the women have watched** (some players never broke through near them before): no "again", "last time", "every time", "as before", no counting of earlier ones they saw. They may know he has climbed before (word gets round); the step's own realm name may be said.
- **No time words** (dawn, night, dark, sun, morning): the same text shows by day and by night. **No ground words** (moss, ash, dust, bark), except T may say "dead bark" (the tree's own floor).
- **Do not mention Yuki** (her line just above covers her, in three forms: a cat, a great root-kin, a woman).
- Mei Lin and Ya show what the step means to people of this world (a sect's elders take centuries for these rungs; Ya cares more about whether it would win a fight; Mei Lin laughs, then means it), and grow plainly more used to the absurdity as the steps rise, without claiming to have seen the earlier ones.

About the same length as the bold words; ends the sentence with a full stop.
