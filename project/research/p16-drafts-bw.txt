rr_elk_won:3 | The bone-elk folds at the knees and goes over sideways, and the root under your boots takes the weight with a groan. Ya has her spear out of it before the antlers stop rattling. ¶ "Hold the leg, scholar," she says. You hold the leg. She works fast and talks faster: where the good meat lies, which cut her people give to the one who struck, and which to the one who stood near and looked clever. You get the second. ¶ A tine has split off against the bark. You pick it up, and your Convergence runs through it like water through limestone, finding every hollow, every seam where the beast grew through a hard winter. It is a record, this bone. A diary of bad years. ¶ You shelve it in the hammerspace, unread past the opening chapter. Ya wipes her knife on her thigh and grins at you.
