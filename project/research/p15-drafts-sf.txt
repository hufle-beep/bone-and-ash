morning:w1 | You wake on the dry rise with a root filed under one kidney and grey moss printed along your cheek. The ground here was not designed with a spine in mind, and yours has spent the night composing a complaint. ¶ Above the black trees the sky goes from ink to rust without consulting anyone. The stream is still talking below the camp. Something small and unhurried is eating the bark of the fallen trunk, and it does not stop on your account. ¶ Yuki is awake already, and regards you as one regards a kettle that is slow to boil. Your knees are asked. They agree to stand, with conditions.
herb_slopes:2:2 | On the upper terrace Mei Lin has you on your knees, working a pale root out of the earth with your fingers, because a blade nicks it and a nicked root rots in the jar. She corrects your grip twice. The second time she is plainly enjoying herself. ¶ "You don't sell them and you don't swallow them," she says, tamping the hole shut with her heel. "So it isn't money and it isn't medicine. I've run out of sensible reasons. Give me a foolish one."
herb_slopes:2:2:bruised | The split in her lip has closed to a thin pale line, which pulls when she grins, and she grins regardless.
night:11:p1 | The forest changes shifts when the dark comes down, and you can now tell the roster by ear. The clicking things in the canopy stop all together. There is a gap, about four breaths long, in which nothing holds the post. Then the low callers start up from the mire side, one, two, the third a little flat.
night:11:p3 | Out on its round, the watch-thread counts the callers in and gets last night's number. It adds them up again anyway, being the one part of you paid to worry.
night:14:p1s2 | You shake the moss out of the coat, lay it down folded, and get your boots off while you can still tell left from right.
night:14:fire | You put your bare feet nearer the fire than is wise and the fire, which has its own opinions on distance, spits once.
night:14:nofire | With no fire lit, the cold finds your bare feet before you do, and you sit on them like a man hiding evidence.
night:14:p3 | The watch-thread takes the word home out to the edge of the rise, where it can keep an eye on it. It raises no objection, which from that quarter is nearly a blessing.
village_night:3:5 | There is no wind, and the banners hang from the tribute post as straight as plumb lines. The lane lies awake over them regardless; a thing that does not move can be watched as long as one likes. The arm that grows bone is out on top of the blanket again, without your having sent it. {yuki} The banners go on doing nothing. Presently, so do you.
