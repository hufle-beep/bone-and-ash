¶1 It is the last day on the slopes before the tribute. She cuts double what she needs and offers no reason. You ask for none. It needs no saying: should tomorrow go wrong, she will have no next season to cut in. Halfway along the terrace, with her back to you and the knife still working, she asks again what a man who needs none of this is doing up here.
¶2 Her father comes up again, briefly. He would walk her up here and get the name of every plant wrong, on purpose, so that she had to put him right.
