# notes
- hk:ya closings name neither Ya nor Yuki, so they hold in every form. hk:ya:2:3 assumes Mei Lin carries a herb basket in the Ashen Hills; hk:ya:2:2 has her touch a dead hawk (warm, not burning).
- hk:2:3:cat: the cat now bats an ember, not a bird; her line kept. It is two sentences (the old string was three).
- ek:win2:3: Ya gets her drop; the shutters do the powder's job, so Mei Lin's pouch stays unthrown. Assumes James is on the arch with Ya and Mei Lin on the ground by the root.
- ek:win3:3: Ya does not drop (outclassed elk, nothing to drop on in earnest); the powder is thrown after the kill, as a joke. Neither elk text has a Yuki sentence.
