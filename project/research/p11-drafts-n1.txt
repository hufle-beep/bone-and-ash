# notes
- Each text's three Yuki passages share their opening words by design (as the week 2 and 3 texts do); between different texts no five-word run is shared, apart from "about the size of your fist" inside text 5's own forms.
- w1:4 assumes sweeping downstairs after supper and that Yuki (human) came to the house with him; "three times your weight" for the great form is my estimate.
- w1:5: the bird behind the woodpile is invented; no species named.
- w1:6 assumes he still has the lab coat in the loft and that one of the hanging herbs could be wormwood (great form only; swap for "the bitter one is for fevers" if canon has no wormwood). "A house where somebody cooks" leans on his love of cooking, not on the supper scene.
