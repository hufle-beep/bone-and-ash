wen:1 | Old Wen takes the forest for his subject and maps it on the table in spilt tea: the oak hollow here, the black slope there, and beyond both a stain he leaves unnamed. The forest, he says, has been wrong for longer than Ashfall has stood, and has used the years to become good at it. What fattens quickly in the forest is to be left where it grows; what comes up slowly may be trusted about as far as the tea has run. "The Sect calls it a garden," he says. "A garden is a place where somebody else decides what lives."
