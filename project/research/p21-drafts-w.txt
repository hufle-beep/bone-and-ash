bluff_win | Lu Han kneels alone in the dust, his disciples gone, and you do not send him anywhere. You step back and let white bone lift out of the earth about the tribute post and stand in a crown, slow, silent, more beautiful than it needs to be. You let the Qi in it reach him: clean, utterly clean, without a grain of the death in everything he has ever eaten. Of its maker his senses still report nothing, and a Foundation disciple knows the one polite reason why they wouldn't. ¶ The still face mends as he does the sum. No mortal put him on his knees, because no mortal can. It follows that none did. ¶ He bows from where he kneels: not deep, exactly deep enough. "This one failed to recognise a senior, and has been corrected," he says. "The Crimson Lotus asks no tribute of Ashfall this season. My master will wish to… extend an invitation." ¶ "I'll check my calendar." ¶ He rises and goes without hurry, as a man leaves a lesson. Far down the road one figure in crimson has stopped to wait for him, and looks back once. The look does not mean *goodbye*.
tribute_paid | Lu Han is still on his knees when you begin to sing, and it takes everything the fight left you: a hairpin the length of your hand, white as moonlight, its leaves turned so fine that the sun comes through them. You have never made anything better. You hold it out to the man you have just beaten as if settling a bill. ¶ He looks at the pin, then at the baskets, then at you, and understands what he is being handed. A disciple thrown out of a village carries home a grievance. A disciple carrying this has only a report to make. ¶ "Ashfall's tribute is paid," he says, and has to start over to get his voice level. "In full. For three years." You slip the pin inside his robe, over his heart, and he lets you. "Whoever you are, Bone-Maker, the Sect will hear of this. This one will see that it hears it from me." ¶ The baskets stay where they stand. Nobody in crimson is in any state to touch one.
fail:bluff | The crown rises crooked, and Lu Han watches it with the calm of a man already beaten. "A trick of bone," he says. "It has cost me enough. It will not also have my respect." He is going regardless.
fail:pin | The song catches on a spent breath, and the pin arrives in two halves, neither of them flawless. Lu Han regards them politely. Ashfall pays nothing, then. Beaten is beaten, though, and he will leave without it.
# notes
- bluff_win and tribute_paid avoid the blade, the broken sword and Lu Han's hands, so they read after collectors_won, win[2] and win[3] alike (in win[3] both his arms are hurt, so James places the pin himself and Lu Han only bows and rises).
- bluff_win's closing figure in crimson is unnamed: it reads as Gao Feng when he is there and as a straggling disciple when he is not. If the build can branch on Gao, "Gao Feng has stopped down the road to wait for him" is the named version.
- Kept from today's texts (both replaced whole): "clean, utterly clean", the "one polite reason" clause, Lu Han's three spoken lines and "I'll check my calendar" in bluff_win; the hairpin's description, "You have never made anything better", "paid … In full. For three years", "Bone-Maker" and the baskets line in tribute_paid. If bluff_fail or the old labels stay in the game, the five-word-run check may flag these.
- tribute_paid: "Pray that it hears it from me" became a promise ("This one will see that…"), since a beaten man no longer threatens. Assumes "this one" is Lu Han's humble form to a victor as well as to a senior.
- title:sent "Neighbour" matches the one-word "Senior" / "Kneel" pattern; "Red Snow" was left for the fight's own screen.
