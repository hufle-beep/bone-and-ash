# Bone and Ash, v0.11.231 drafts, run A (owner pick P4-2, AT: the Ashen Hills camp while a woman is away)

You draft story texts for a hand-written text RPG. Read ONLY this brief and `/home/claude/bone-and-ash/project/canon-card.md` (canon, tone, the tic list). Do not read other repo files.

Tone: James and Yuki deadpan. Second person ("you" = James). Locals overly serious; Mei Lin looser (teases, laughs); Ya (a wolf-tribe spearwoman) blunt and physical. Yuki, his bonded white cat, has three forms: the cat (speaks only through the bond, in italics `*like this*`; rides on his shoulder), the great root-kin (a tiger-sized white cat, also bond only; never "on your shoulder"), the woman (speaks aloud, in quotes). Where a line names her look, her place or her speech, write all three forms; otherwise leave her out or keep her form-free.

## Context

Act 1, the Ashen Hills: black ridges, ash, grey sky, one dead giant tree over everything. No grass, no living trees, no ferns. James, Yuki, Mei Lin (herbalist) and Ya travel together and camp here for some days. On some days, in the daytime only, a woman is away from camp:
- **Ya away** (Mei Lin is there): Ya has walked off in a sulk with her spear.
- **Both away**: Mei Lin and Ya have gone off together for the day.
They are back by evening. The lines below show in the daytime on those days and name a woman who is not there. Write versions as each key asks:
- `any`: one version with NEITHER woman in it (it shows in both states).
- `ya`: Ya not in it; Mei Lin may be (she is there).
- `both`: neither woman in it.

Rules: same idea, the same success or failure, the same length (give or take a sentence). Don't say where the women are, that they are away, or why. Solitude may be noted at most twice across all your lines. Avoid images of paperwork, rulings, reports, objections, drafts, instalments, courts or clerks (the game overuses them). Ash-country scenery only. British spelling. No song lyrics, no real names.
