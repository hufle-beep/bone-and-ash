## 1. hs:honest:2: "Answer her question. Honestly." picked on a later visit
On every slopes visit Mei Lin asks, one way or another, why James is up here (see the packet's texts: "Why are you here?", "asks again what a man who needs none of this is doing up here", "I've run out of sensible reasons. Give me a foolish one."). Choice [1], `Answer her question. Honestly.`, always gives the same reply:
`"Because you're here," you say. You hear it, are appalled, and add: "The grass is also fascinating." She laughs until her legs give up and she sits down in it. One terrace over, an old herb-woman unbends and fixes her with a stare until the laughing stops. The stare is needed for a while.`
That reply stays for the first time a Coil. Write the reply for every later time in the same Coil (it can follow any of her texts, any week, and be read more than once): he has already given her that answer, and both know it. 30–50 words. Mei Lin looser (teases, laughs), James deadpan. Don't reuse "appalled", the grass being fascinating, her sitting down, or the old herb-woman's stare.

Key: `hs:honest:2`.

## 2. w2:2:clause: the bracket fungus
Week 2's second quiet night (in `B-vn.txt`) opens: `You are classifying a bracket fungus from the forest edge and getting nowhere. Grey on top, violet beneath, growing on wood that should have killed it; nothing you were taught has a name for it, and your mind keeps offering names regardless.`
"Grey on top, violet beneath, on wood" describes Earth's commonest violet-pored bracket, which any mycologist would name at once; his world is not Earth. Replace only `Grey on top, violet beneath, growing on wood that should have killed it` (up to the semicolon) with a description no Earth species matches: still a bracket on wood, still **grey on top** (Yuki answers "Call it the grey one"), plus one or two marks a mycologist would find impossible to place. 10–25 words, no Latin, no real species.

Key: `w2:2:clause` (just the replacement words, no semicolon).

## 3. say:holdNowMany: the hold line when two or more foes are winding up a speech
In a fight, "Say something" (a dry remark) cuts short a foe's speech, the big blow he is posturing for: that foe loses this round's attack and the next. James is not guarding while he says it. The line shown under the choice on such a round is `holdNow` in `B-say.txt`: `A speech is coming this round: say something and it never finishes.` It reads as if one speaker. On some rounds two or three foes posture at once (Lu Han and Gao Feng; the Sect's collectors; the arsonists), and a remark stops only one of them; the others still attack, and he is unguarded. Write the line for those rounds: one or two short sentences, 15–28 words, the same dry rules voice as the other lines in `B-say.txt`, no `{foe}` slot, no numbers. It must say that a remark stops one speech only, and that the other speakers still swing at an unguarded man.

Key: `say:holdNowMany`.
