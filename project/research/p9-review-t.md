## Medium

**M1. The Ashen Hills' third camp puts the hawks over the camp on the hawk night.** "The cinder hawks have come down to roost along the ridge above the camp, each under its own wisp of smoke." This shows only on AH night 3, which is the story night. `NIGHTS.ah` = hawks, stars, lights, warm, hawks, mei: hawks come up in 2 of 6 rolls before the re-roll. In about 1/3 of games the screen sets up the attack ("Cinder hawks hunt at night as well, you learn"), so the "you learn" is given away. In the other 2/3, predators the game framed as hunting Yuki ("Take the hawks before they take Yuki.", `prefersSmall`) sleep a spear's throw above the camp while Ya, the huntress, idly counts them. Fable's brief said the text "can't promise quiet or danger". The hub just before it reads "cinder hawks circle, each trailing a thin thread of smoke", and "each under its own wisp of smoke" repeats that construction on the next screen. Suggested fix (checked with `--check`, no tic or run notes): *"Along the far ridge the cinder hawks have gone to roost, a row of small smokes against the grey. The routine has settled: packs strapped shut, the water covered, and Mei Lin shaking the ash out of her sleeves while Ya counts the smokes and loses her place."*
