## TB, the tier beats (a closing paragraph, once a Coil, on the next carrier after the tier)
**Mei Lin 30 (the kiss tier), at supper**
Under the table, Mei Lin's foot comes to rest on top of yours and stays there. Above the table she is a dutiful daughter, asking her mother whether the well rope will see out the month. You keep your face in order, which is the one skill your education did give you, and leave your foot where it is. The rope will do, her mother rules. Mei Lin presses down once, lightly, as one presses a seal onto a letter, and goes on being a credit to the house.

**Mei Lin 72 (liked), at supper**
Mei Lin is humming, and the tune belongs to you. It gets out of your forearm when you are not minding it, quietly, and she has it nearly right, down to where the drummer loses patience. She does not seem to know she is doing it. Her mother knows. She hears out a whole verse with her hands folded, looks at her daughter, and then at you, for about the time it takes to price a goat. "That is not a song of this lane," she says. "See that you learn how it ends." Mei Lin stops, colours, and starts over, louder.

**Ya 45 (her kiss tier), at a Blackroot camp**
Ya picks up her sleeping hide, spear and all, puts it down hard against your bedroll, and sits on it to look at you. "I have thought about it, scholar," she says, loud enough for the whole camp. "I want you. I am telling you so that you are not surprised." You thank her for the notice. Over the flames Mei Lin asks whether Ya would care to put that in writing. Ya, who cannot write, says writing is for people who forget what they want, and goes on looking.

**Ya 72 (liked), at a Blackroot camp**
Ya wets her thumb, rubs it in the cold char by the fire, and draws a line under your eye, where her own scar runs. She is not flirting; she does it as she would check a spearhead, frowning, and leans away to judge the work. "There. Now what is out there knows whose you are." You ask what that makes you. "Mine," she says. "Not to eat. To keep. It is different, scholar. Ask the herb girl, she has the words." Mei Lin tells you it is crooked. Nobody offers to wipe it off, and you leave it there.

**Ya 45, the Fallout loft night (she is on the shrine roof)**
Ya, over on the shrine's ridge, sees you looking and does not duck. She stands up instead, tall on the skyline, points the spear at your window, then lays her free hand flat on her own chest and waits to be understood. It is not a subtle language. You raise one hand, which commits you to nothing, and she sits down satisfied, as a hunter does who has marked where the deer beds.

**Ya 72, the Fallout loft night**
While you look, the figure over the lane turns its back on your window and settles facing the road, spear laid over its knees. It takes you a little while to read it. Ya was watching you; now she is watching for you, as she would sit over her own fire and her own kin. You have, without any paperwork, been moved from quarry to household. The loft feels smaller, and better guarded.

**talk_mei 1**

**talk_mei 2**
There was a Mei Lin who knew you snore, and told the whole camp so, with evidence. The Coil took her and the evidence both. This one still thinks well of you on that point, and you let her.

**talk_mei 3**
You know what she said into your sleeve when she believed you were asleep, on a Coil she has no part in now. She would deny every word, and she would be right. You carry it for the two of you; it weighs very little, and you notice it at every step.

## MC-A (the owner 20:03): run MC, three more beats at the camps
Cuts (PG): `mei30:camp` Fable's redraft (the first draft was about James's glasses); three five-word runs reworded.

**Mei Lin 30, at a Blackroot camp**
Mei Lin takes the waterskin out of your hand before you have stoppered it and drinks from it as it is. In Ashfall a girl wipes the neck with her sleeve before she drinks after a man, and makes sure she is seen doing it; Mei Lin explains this to you afterwards, pleasantly, as a point of local custom, with the skin not yet lowered. Her eyes stay on yours while she says it. Ya snorts. Mei Lin hands the skin back, neck towards you, and waits to see what a man of learning does with it. You drink. It seemed the scholarly course.

**Mei Lin 72, at a Blackroot camp**
There is a tear in your coat cuff, and Mei Lin is mending it with you still inside the coat, which leaves your hand in her lap and nothing for you to do about it. She sews slowly, for a woman who is quick with a needle. Ya watches from the other side of the fire, chin on her spear shaft. "You make that seam long, herb girl." Mei Lin says some work should not be hurried, and does not look up. Ya grunts, scowls at the flames, and then holds out the frayed cord of her tooth necklace. "Mine after. Not so slow." Mei Lin takes it.

**Ya 72, at the camp inside the Old Tree**
Ya strides out to where the firelight gives up, plants her spear, and addresses the dark of the hall in her wolf voice. The scholar, she informs it, sleeps inside her fire and eats from her kills, and is not to be taken by anything. Whatever wants him may come and discuss it with her. The hall makes no answer. You remark that nobody is there. "Then nobody has heard it clearly," Ya says, and comes back to sit close against you, pleased with the evening's work. Mei Lin, from her watch, promises to inform any callers.

**Ya 72, at the camp inside the Old Tree (redraft; places nobody, so it sits under Ya's watch or the heap)**
Ya counts what belongs to her, aloud, a finger for each: the spear, the knife, the teeth at her throat, the fire, and then, with a look at you, the scholar.
