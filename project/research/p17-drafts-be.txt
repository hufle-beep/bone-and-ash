win2:2 | Mid-charge, you ask the left antler to put on weight. It obliges, thickening to millstone heft, and the elk's head tips, and the charge bends into a long helpless curve that ends against a buttress of the root. ¶ Ya's spear settles what is left, one thrust under the ribs, and wastes none of the elk's time. ¶ "It ran *crooked*," she says, wiping the point. "You made it drunk in the horns, scholar. Do me next. No. Do the herb girl." ¶ "I heard that," says Mei Lin, from behind the root. You put a chipped tine away in the hammerspace. It weighs more than the elk ever meant it to.
win2:3 | The elk comes on head down, and you have a word with the brow tines. They broaden, flatten and fold across its eyes, two neat white shutters, and the elk goes by you at full gallop with no idea where anybody is. ¶ Ya knows where it is. Her spear takes it in passing, low behind the foreleg, and it is dead before it stops sliding. ¶ "You shut its eyes for it," she says, knocking on the bone with a knuckle. "Like a lid on a pot. Meanest thing I ever saw, scholar. Show me." ¶ Behind the root, Mei Lin is laughing too hard to come out.
win3:2 | The elk lowers its rack and comes, and you suggest to the rack that the season has turned. Both antlers let go at the burr and drop into the moss behind it, like a traveller setting down bags. ¶ What reaches you is a bare, astonished forehead. You stop it with the flat of one hand. Ya's spear does the rest from the side, before the beast can work out what it has lost. ¶ "I *said* I wanted the skull," she tells you. "With the horns *on*, scholar." ¶ Mei Lin leans round the root. "He did leave you the face." Ya considers this, and you, and starts to grin.
win3:3 | You don't move. The antlers do: every tine leans left together, as wheat leans in wind, and the elk's head has to go with them, and the elk with its head. It trots a tidy circle round you, furious, and cannot find the way out. ¶ "That is not a hunt," Ya says, spear lowered, deeply wronged. "That is a goat on a rope." She takes it on the next pass all the same, one thrust and done. ¶ Mei Lin looks over the root. "Does it go the other direction?" ¶ Ya wipes her spear and eyes you sidelong. "What else do you lead about like that, scholar?"
# notes
- Assumed Ya kills with her spear here (the given texts use her knife at the throat); swap to the knife if canon wants it.
- win2:2 names one tine going into the hammerspace, as the given texts do; the other three leave the gain to the screen. Cut that sentence if the tine should not be named.
- win3:2: "burr" is the ring where an antler joins the skull; "at the base" works if it reads as too technical. Ya says "horns" on purpose (her word, not James's).
- win3:2 assumes James can stop the bare-headed elk with one hand (the opening's "You have stopped carts").
- No Yuki in any of the four.
