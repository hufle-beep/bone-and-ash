# Group i round 2, wave 2, cluster H: the big non-idle cultivation, xianxia and wuxia games (read-only research, 2026-11-08)

Read for Bone and Ash (v0.11.75): a story-heavy, turn-based-combat, cultivation loop game for one phone. Every game here is closed source: **ideas only**, described in my own words. Two open mod repos for The Scroll of Taiwu were cloned (licences below); nothing in them is game design we could take as code. Every "Bone and Ash today" cell was checked in `js/core`, `js/story`, `js/main.js`.

## 3. Considered and skipped

- **Generations and heirs** (Taiwu's passing the sword, legacy points; Immortal Family Simulator): wave 1 skipped inheritance by generation; romance fades to black; the Coil already carries James across. Taiwu's legacy points read as weak and random even to its players (Qieyou).
- **Lifespan as the player's clock** (觅长生, Tale of Immortal): the acts' deadlines are the clock; lifespan stays flavour for locals (H3).
- **Simulated NPC lives every month** (Tale of Immortal, Taiwu): players call Tale of Immortal's a "paper tiger"; dated, written beats give the story (H3, #59).
- **A yearly favour drift**: wave 1 skipped decay; only its shape is used (see #61).
- **A grudge ledger for beaten foes**: already threaded by flags (`gao_broken` read in 12 places, `gao_spared` in 7).
- **A seclusion card in the acts**: "Cultivate until the limit" (v0.11.64) and the Long Forest's season seclusion (`lf_seclusion`) exist.
- **Failure that teaches** (觅长生's failed Nascent Soul raising stats): Intuitive Aptitude already eases the next try.
- **A mood bar** (Legend of Mortal's 心相): one more bar beside Fed and HP; sprawl.
- **Breakthrough mini-games** (Taiwu's board with % tiles): odds, save-scumming and a fiddly phone screen.
- **Lasting wounds after a lost story fight** (Tale of Immortal, six months): already planned as injuries (section 4).
- **Open-world tiles, bullet-hell action, colony management, feng shui, hex grids, five-element matching, PvP cards** (Tale of Immortal, ACS, Path of Wuxia, Yi Xian, Cultivation Story): don't fit a text story game on a phone.
- **Material grinds for each realm** (Tale of Immortal's orbs, treasures, bottleneck pills): James needs no herbs or pills by design.

## 4. Avoid list (seen there)

- **Random breakthrough odds** invite save-scumming: Tale of Immortal's guides say "save before, reload if poor"; a Taiwu 1.0 mod exists only because save-and-load "makes you tired"; Legend of Mortal was review-bombed partly for dice-driven difficulty. Bone and Ash's deterministic checks are right; keep them.
- **Hidden numbers** that need theft or mods to read (Tale of Immortal's favour; Taiwu's MoreInfo / ShowMeMore / NpcScan mods): show status in words (#114).
- **Grind as the late game**: Tale of Immortal's repetitive quests and bigger-number monsters (GCORES); "hellishly grindy" breakthrough materials (Something Awful).
- **Requirements you learn about too late** (Tale of Wuxia), or missable windows with no hint.
- **Boss fights that only out-of-character tricks win** (Hero's Adventure's Inner Demon).
- **No saves in key sequences, no carry-over between plays, unfinished routes** (Legend of Mortal's launch): never break a save; every route finished; the Coil keeps what it promises.
- **Permadeath that deletes the save** (Tale of Immortal's hardest mode).
- **Too many stats** (Legend of Mortal 11+, Tale of Wuxia 20+): keep GU, Convergence, Fed, HP.
