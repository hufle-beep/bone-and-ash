## Context the packet doesn't show
Act 1: James, Mei Lin and Ya walk through the Blackroot to the Old Tree (story days 11–15). Three places, and a talk can happen in any of them: the Root Road (a root as broad as a cart track through a black forest), the Drowned Wood (flooded trees, rafts, a marsh) and the Ashen Hills (ash, haze, ridges, cinder hawks, no trees). On the long calendar (the game's default) each story day lasts a WEEK, and the player can talk to each woman once a day: the Sociable player talks to each 12–15 times a game. After five one-off beats, each talk plays a cycle: today it is three texts (the quiet walk, A, B), so a player reads them four or five times each. You write three more for each woman, so the cycle is six.

Rules for the new texts:
- **Place-neutral:** no trees, roots, water, ash or haze in a new text (it must stand in all three places). "The path", "the way", the ground, the light, the camp behind, the walking are fine. (Ya-away versions are always in the Ashen Hills, so the haze is fine there.)
- **No day counts and no time words** ("tomorrow", "this morning", "tonight", "today", "at dusk"): a talk shows between 06:00 and 19:00 on any day of the week.
- **Not the beats:** don't retell the five beats or A, B and the quiet walk (see `tk-texts.txt`); each new text is a new small moment of the walk: something she asks, does, notices, teases, admits. No romance beyond the existing warm branches (a touch, a look, a hand; the scenes past them fade at the door and live elsewhere). Don't assume that a romance scene has happened.
- **The fold:** a text read before folds to its FIRST SENTENCE with a "Read it all again" link. So the base paragraph's first sentence must stand alone as a reminder of the moment, and every base paragraph has at least two sentences.
- **Yuki:** leave her out (if you must name her, give all three forms: the cat on his shoulder, bond-speech in italics; the great root-kin, tiger-sized, bond-speech in italics; the woman with cat ears, speaks aloud).
- **The choices stay** and must still make sense after each text: Mei Lin: [0] `Listen. Ask questions. Let her talk.` (Mei Lin +), [1] `Flirt. Lightly. Dangerously.` (her result: a reply that she calls it impossible, or that it carries a fine in Ashfall); Ya: [0] `Flirt back.
