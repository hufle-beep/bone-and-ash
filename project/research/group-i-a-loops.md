## 3. Considered and skipped

- **Need a queue or a timer (gone since v0.11.64):** Idle Loops' predictor, loadouts and pause options; Cavernous II's routes (the Pareto front), clone sync, grind routes and saved queues; wrtsc's queue editor; Timestream's Sequence Buffer and Safety Stasis; Chrono's "Planificador Vital" (when X, do Y; Selene's preset triggers); Immortality Idle's pause on battle and pause on a failed impossible task; Reworked's offline time, overclock and fatigue pause.
- **Random rolls (Bone and Ash has no dice):** Immortality Idle's generated techniques and refinements; Incremental Chronicles' best-of-three combat (`randf()` against a strength ratio); Idle Loops' soulstone chance; Idle Fishing's random Mastery offers (a fixed version is the "thread's domain" row); Chrono's Eureka bursts.
- **Doesn't fit a story game on a phone:** Idle Fishing's real-time catch minigame (`ActiveFishingModal.svelte`); Immortality Idle's homes, furniture, upkeep and land, followers who age, draw wages and leave when unpaid, and its HQ; Chrono's rent, deposit and eviction; Timestream's UI theme that changes with each era.
- **Sprawl or prestige layers (Selene's avoid list):** Immortality Idle's yin-yang, elements, formations, divinity, hell, pantheon and contemplation concepts; Progress Knight's evil and Progress Knight Quest's five layers, challenges, events and dark matter; Chrono's Karma tree, keystones and latent Karma; Timestream's compounding multipliers (1.01^Mastery × 1.05^Focus) and compounding decay (+20% a minute).
- **Already there or planned:** the Cavernous II stalemate chip (Bone and Ash has DR and the 30-round cap); wrtsc's combat as an auto task ("End it in one move"); Cavernous II's first-time messages (the unfolding FAQ); Cavernous II's kept loop logs and Immortality Idle's life summary and statistics (the Coil screen, and group h's #20 history of past Coils); Incremental Chronicles' texts by exact rebirth count (`Reincarnation: appear min/max`; the planned "again" versions, P1-b, are the same thing), its pinned requirement list (the next-step line) and its hidden `h_` flags (knowledge flags); Chrono's requirements re-checked every life with a permanent "discovered" flag for the UI (world flags against knowledge flags); Immortality Idle's ice shield and survival formation, which block one lethal blow (the Ouroboros daily knockout beat, planned); Cavernous II's damage that stays until healed, no healing during a fight (as now); Immortality Idle's death-cause lines (`checkForDeath`; Coils already end with a cause).
- **Numbers that would move every fight:** wrtsc's armour curve (offense ÷ (offense + defense))^1.5; the game's small numbers work with flat DR and ratio bands.
- **Progress Knight's auto-promote** (there is no job ladder); its auto-learn lives on only as the "learning" thread's rule.
- **Reworked's tutorial zone** (the prologue is the tutorial).

## 4. Avoid list

- **Sprawl:** Immortality Idle's game state alone is 38.5k lines in 25 services, every system added and none retired. One system in, one decision on what it replaces.
- **Bonuses farmed by resetting at once.** Bone and Ash has one today (the Coil bonus counts bites at dawn; see the candidate above). Every carry-over should come from what a Coil actually did.
- **Carry-overs that feed themselves during the same run** (Chrono's bug #4): settle them at the rewind.
- **Text and code drifting apart** (Chrono's bug #9: a tooltip said +50%, the code gave +7%): take shown numbers from the data, and test that they match.
- **A companion lost or killed by a mechanic** (Reworked's "skull medal": the squirrel dies doing the action). Yuki never leaves; her versions only add.
- **Showing every locked text's condition at once:** Idle Loops shows only the next one in a branch and "???" for the rest; keep the mystery.
- **Rolls in fights or loot, and per-frame or real-time rewards** (Chrono's bug #8, Idle Fishing's minigame).
- **Treadmill economies:** wages as a share of income, followers leaving when unpaid, rent and eviction.
- **Obfuscated code and hidden data** (Progress Knight Quest decodes data from its logo image): copy nothing from it.
- **Fan IP:** Timestream Chronicles is a Doctor Who fan game; no names or setting.
