roof:1:4 | Mei Lin asks, with some formality, what your name means, since in Ashfall a name is expected to mean something. ¶ "It's old. It comes out of a language nobody speaks any more." You have to hunt for the rest. "One who takes another's place. A supplanter." ¶ She turns this over gravely, her eyes on the roofs below. "That is not a name. That is an accusation. Whose place did you take?" ¶ "Nobody's, so far. My parents liked the sound." ¶ "They hung a theft on a child because it sounded well." She seems greatly cheered. "Mine is a beautiful forest." She nods towards the black slope above the village, where the grey moss hangs. "You have walked through the forest." ¶ "Then neither of us is as described." "I am still deciding about you," she says, with great courtesy, to the lane.
roof:1:4:stay | The cold decides it before she does. On the ladder she tries the meaning aloud as a title, and finds that it suits a lodger rather well. Indoors she wishes the supplanter goodnight, bowing slightly, with a perfectly straight face. You climb to the herb loft and take the place of nobody, among the drying mint.
