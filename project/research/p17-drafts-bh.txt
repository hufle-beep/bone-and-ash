# notes
- win2:2: Ya knots one of James's bone darts onto her tooth necklace ("Scholar's tooth"); assumes the darts are solid and stay (the first text has him collect them). "Closer to a rib, if it is anything" is James being dry, not a claim about where Convergence bone comes from; cut the sentence if canon minds.
- win3:2 and its away version: the lattice closes round the hawks in mid-air as a cage, and can be drawn in; win3:3: it drops as a lid; win3:3 away: strung between two glass ridges like a net. Assumes Convergence can shape and move a lattice this freely (the first text has it open overhead at a raised finger).
- Hawk supper: none of these say what happens to the birds afterwards, so the gains stay untouched.
- Main versions name Ya only (no Mei Lin), so they hold whether or not Mei Lin is there; away versions name no woman.
- Human form: "her hem" assumes the qipao of the canon card.
- "Three" appears only as the number of hawks, as in the first texts.
