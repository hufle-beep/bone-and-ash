# PL drafts, part 2 (v0.11.189, D1; Fable, run 2, about 63k tokens; word for word)

### PG
"One thunder," Ya says. Mei Lin asks what this one is called. You tell her. She repeats it, as she would a price. "If the Sect heard that, it would bar its gates and claim to be a farm." "Does it help in a fight?" Ya asks. "It hasn't come up," you say.

### MG
"What is it called?" Mei Lin says. You tell her. She starts to say it back and laughs instead. "No. I am not saying that aloud. My mother would hear it from Ashfall and come to ask what I had done." She looks the way Ya went. "She will say you made it up."

### TG
Mei Lin comes up the crack and looks you over for holes. "One thunder. We nearly didn't climb." Ya hauls herself onto the dead bark. "Name it, scholar." You name it. Ya tries the words, frowns, and gives them back. "Too long to shout in a fight. I'll keep 'scholar'." "It's shorter," you agree.
