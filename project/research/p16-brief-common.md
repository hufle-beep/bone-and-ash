- Your texts are later entries of a sequence: each is read on a later day than the ones you are given, and stands alone. **No counts** ("the third time", "again and again", "every day now"), no "first" / "last" about the day, no promises about a later day, no foreshadowing.
- Time words: no clock times; no "late", "midnight", "dawn", "tomorrow" unless the brief allows one.
