# notes
- rr:2: now an after-supper quarrel; no first taste, no pot-scraping (the cook reply has it). Yuki alone counts.
- ah:2:none is by form (:cat, :great, :woman); the three share their wording on purpose, as the rr:2 forms do.
- ah:2:close picks up the open's "a colour that nobody mentions"; it has no bowl in it, so it fits the variants where Ya is at the spear.
- ah:3:mei: the teeth lead into "What does a tree that size eat?".
- cw:supper: the asker is now the dyer's wife, so no "he" follows Mei Lin's "He knows"; a rumour only, he has not cooked here.
- cw:mei_walk goes on text 3 (the looser one); "tows" because the sentence before ends "steering by it".
- roof 1:3: the music is brisk; "merely loud" still answers it.
- ya 9:2: "sixty years" replaces "eighty years" in her reply too; chaff became dust.
