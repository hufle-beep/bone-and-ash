# PL notes (Fable, run 1; suggestions only)


- **Grouped steps are written weather-blind.** C, D and F each cover realms whose sky behaves differently (full storm / one cloud / a bolt a mile off / a bolt that lands / no lightning), so those texts say only that the sky came and left, never clouds, strokes or scorch. A and B name their own weather lightly ("the storm", "the lightning").
- **Realm names:** the women cannot sense his realm, so wherever a name is used he says it or one of them asks. C/D/F never print a name ("Say the name." "You say it."); PF/MF/TF use only "Immortal", which is in all three names of realms 12-14.
- **Assumptions to check against the game:**
  - PC: Ya has seen James fly ("You go up like a thrown rock"). If a player can reach realm 4-5 without ever having flown near her, swap that sentence.
  - PF: "I have watched you apologise to a mushroom" is invented (fits the mycologist; no scene behind it that I know of).
  - PA/MA: "They get carried places", "we kneel to them", "older than my grandmother's grandmother" are Mei Lin's colour about outer elders; Shen Qiu is not named, and no number of years is given.
  - PC/MC/TC: "banners", "a peak, with a crowd", "within a field of one" are invented sect-master ceremony.
  - MD: "carved over temple doors" assumes temples exist (the supper text has a shrine).
  - M texts say "the camp" only; the hills are not named (the ground-word rule would catch "Ashen").
- **James closers:** PA, PC, PD, MA, MD, ME, TC, TE, TF (9 of 18).
- **Lengths:** all aimed at 45-55 words; MA and TE are the longest and worth a script count.
- **K2/K3:** both keep "informing the ladle that" so the sentence joins as before. K2's "these days" is the only time-ish phrase; the K2 alt has none.
