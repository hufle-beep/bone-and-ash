## RH, the bone-elk (Root Road), 4th
**rr_hunt:4**
A bone-elk stands square on the Root Road, drinking from a split in the bark where rainwater has pooled, and the one who sees it is Mei Lin, stopped to pick a burr off her sleeve.

Grey hide, and the bone wound down from its skull in pale coils that click together as it swallows. Your Convergence follows each click, and would like it known that it could do better. Yuki has gone entirely still.

"Mine," Ya breathes. "I go under the root and come up at its belly. Scholar, you nail its feet to the road. Herb girl holds the sheath of my spear."

"Your spear has no sheath," Mei Lin whispers.

"Then your hands are free. Clap when I win."


## HK, the hawks' ordinary win
**hawks1:4**
The cinder hawks lie cooling on the black glass, each in a crater of its own digging, and the ridge smells of singed pillow.

{yuki}

Mei Lin is already plucking one, though it is still hot. "A proper girl never handles game," she says, blowing on her fingers. "Mother was very clear." Ya watches her technique in visible pain.
- *cat:* They had stooped at Yuki, one after another, and found your darts instead. She leans from your collar to look them over. *Stringy,* she says. *I am doubly insulted.*
- *great:* The hawks had circled wide of Yuki as ships do a reef, and come at you. She noses the nearest one over. *Cowards cook the same,* she says.
- *human:* The hawks had stooped at Yuki in turn, and she had leaned aside exactly as far as needed. "Stringy," she says, turning one over with her foot. "I am worth better."

**hawks1:4:away**
The cinder hawks are down, strewn along the black glass where each one's dive ended, still ticking as they cool.

{yuki}

You pick up what you have been allotted. It is still hot enough to mind.
- *cat:* Yuki, whom every dive was meant for, drops to the ash and inspects each in turn. *This one,* she decides. *The rest are yours.*
- *great:* Yuki, whom no dive had dared come near, paces along the row and halts over the fattest. *Mine. It looked at me,* she says. *You may have the others.*
- *human:* Yuki, at whom each of them had aimed, goes down the row with her hands clasped behind her. "That one is mine," she says. "Divide what remains among yourself."

**hawks1:5**
The fight ends with burning birds falling out of a grey sky, and you stepping politely aside to let them land.

{yuki}

Ya is walking between the birds, stamping out embers with her bare feet and no change of expression. "Hot," she concedes at length, to nobody. Then, to you: "You dodge like a man avoiding work, scholar. I like watching it."
- *cat:* Yuki, the reason for every one of those dives, has a singed patch behind one ear and is pretending otherwise. *It was like that before,* she says.
- *great:* Yuki, whom the birds flew round at a respectful distance, shakes ash from her coat over everybody. *They were afraid,* she says. *Quite correctly.*
- *human:* Yuki, at whom all of that was aimed, has a scorch on one sleeve and has folded her arms over it. "The sleeve was always so," she says.

**hawks1:5:away**
The sky above the ridge is clear of cinder hawks, and the ash below it is not.

{yuki}

You do as you are told. It saves time.
- *cat:* Yuki, whom they had wanted so badly, rides your collar down the slope to see what became of them. *Pick up the left one,* she says. *It was the rudest.*
- *great:* Yuki, whom every bird had steered clear of, goes ahead down the slope and lies across the warmest patch. *Bring them here,* she says. *I am not moving.*
- *human:* Yuki, whom they had preferred to you, walks down the slope ahead and points with her chin. "Begin with the left one," she says. "It was the rudest."


## VM, the village mornings
**vm:3:2**
The red banners hang on the tribute post, and Ashfall has agreed not to look at them. It does this in plain view, all morning, with buckets.

From the door of Mei Lin's house you watch a man carry one bucket past the post in both directions, eyes front. {yuki}
- *cat:* *He isn't looking with real conviction,* says Yuki, on your shoulder. *I have ignored you for forty years. He could ask me.*
- *great:* *He isn't looking with real conviction,* says Yuki, filling the passage behind you. *I have ignored you for forty years. He could ask me.*
- *human:* "He isn't looking with real conviction," says Yuki, at your elbow. "I have ignored you for forty years. He could ask me."

**vm:3:2:burned**
Behind you the scorched loft ticks as its charred beams warm, the one thing in Ashfall that is not pretending.

**vm:3:3**
Breakfast is a quiet meal in Mei Lin's house this week. Her mother sets the bowls down without a sound, as if the banners up the street could hear crockery.

Mei Lin clinks her spoon on purpose. Nobody's eyes rise, and the room cools. {yuki}
- *cat:* Yuki purrs under the table, loudly enough to count as an accomplice, and is excused it, rank having its uses.
- *great:* Yuki, who does not fit under the table, purrs beside it like a millstone, and is excused it, rank having its uses.
- *human:* Yuki sets her own spoon down with a click. It is received as a pronouncement, and the mother inclines her head to it.

**vm:3:3:burned**
Soot from the scorched loft has settled along the table's edge, and the mother wipes it off between mouthfuls, saying nothing.

**vm:3:4**
Somebody has limed the tribute post white under its red banners, and the smell of wet lime comes through the shutters of Mei Lin's house. It is the brightest thing in Ashfall, and the least loved.

- *cat:* *Three men, one gate, nothing wrong with it,* says Yuki from the sill. *I approve. It is how I guard you.*
- *great:* *Three men, one gate, nothing wrong with it,* says Yuki, filling the window beside you. *I approve. It is how I guard you.*
- *human:* "Three men, one gate, nothing wrong with it," says Yuki, leaning out beside you. "I approve. It is how I guard you."

**vm:3:4:burned**
These shutters are the scorched loft's, charred down one edge, and they leave your palm black, as if you had signed something.

**vm:1:2**
You wake in the loft to a bundle of drying wormwood an inch from your nose. Mei Lin hung it there, and you suspect she measured.

Below, the house is already up and being quiet at you. Ashfall has not settled what a tall stranger in a white coat is for; until it does, doors along the lane close a little as you come and open once you have passed.

{yuki}
- *cat:* *They think you're a physician, a ghost or a tax,* says Yuki, from the rafters. *I told them nothing. I'm a cat.*
- *great:* *They think you're a physician, a ghost or a tax,* says Yuki, who has the floor, all of it. *I'd settle it, but I'm asleep.*
- *human:* "They think you're a physician, a ghost or a tax," says Yuki, by the hatch. "I said harmless. They bowed and disbelieved me."

**vm:1:3**

{yuki}

- *cat:* *Beam,* says Yuki, who watched you walk into it and held her peace. *I'd have warned you, but I was curious.*
- *great:* *Beam,* says Yuki, from her half of the loft, which is most of it. *I'd have warned you, but I was curious.*
- *human:* "Beam," says Yuki, who clears it by a head and watched you go. "I'd have warned you, but I was curious."

## HM, the hairpin supper (swaps only)
**supper_w3:5**
Before the bowls are filled, Mei Lin is made to sit with her back to her mother, and the hairpin comes out. It goes back in bare, and its red tassel lies between the salt and the greens.

"A thing that swings is looked at," the mother says. "This week, nothing in this house is looked at."

"It's a tassel. It's mine."

"Then it will be yours again when they have gone." She serves you, then herself, and her daughter after both, which is not the order of this table.

Mei Lin eats watching the tassel.

{yuki}
- *cat:* When Mei Lin's mother rises for the pot, Mei Lin ties the tassel round Yuki's neck. The mother comes back, finds red on a spirit-beast, and rules, after thought, that this lies outside her authority.
- *great:* When Mei Lin's mother rises for the pot, Mei Lin knots the tassel into the fur at Yuki's throat, one berry in a snowfield. The mother comes back, finds red on a spirit-beast, and rules, after thought, that this lies outside her authority.
- *human:* When Mei Lin's mother rises for the pot, Mei Lin slides the tassel along the table, and Yuki ties it round her own wrist. "Mine," she says, when the mother comes back, and is bowed to, since nobody instructs the honoured one in dress.


## LF (LC1), the roof's lines after a burn
**roof_stay:3:0:burned**
The cold finds her hands before the rest of her. She takes the rungs with her fingertips, to spare the wood, and leaves a green thumbprint on your cuff anyway, which she studies and does not apologise for. You carry it into the scorched loft, where water makes no impression on it either.

**roof_stay:3:1:burned**
When the cold comes she smooths the hollow flat with her palm, as one does a bed, and is first down. On the ladder she says he snored up there, and the whole lane agreed to call it cultivation. You reach the scorched loft feeling calmer and no stronger, which she would say runs in the roof.

**roof_stay:3:2:burned**
You stay until the cold has won the argument. On the ladder she remarks, loudly enough for the house, that it looks a settled night, and the house keeps its opinion to itself. Then she bows you goodnight, correctly, and you go up to the loft and its charred beams.
