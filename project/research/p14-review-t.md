## H

The new forest nights give the forest a dusk; the scene's own texts say it has none.** Old nights: 1 "Night on this world is not light going away", 3 "without the courtesy of a dusk", 5 "Dusk is not a thing this forest does", 7 "as if the day had been a clerical error, now corrected", 2/4/6/8 the dark "comes down" ("all at once", "all of a piece"). New: 10 "Here the dark comes up, not down… fills the forest like water filling a basin" (gradual), 11 "Before the light goes", 12 "The light leaves the… sky by degrees", 14 "with the light going", 15 "as the light goes": 5 of 6 (old: 0 of 9). *Failure:* night 15 ends "as the light goes"; night 16 folds to old 1's first paragraph, "Night on this world is not light going away." Nights 17, 18 and 20 fold to "The dark comes down the way it did before", "without the courtesy of a dusk", "Dusk is not a thing this forest does", a few nights after 10, 12 and 14 said otherwise. Cause: the N brief asks for "the dark arriving" in ¶1 and bans "Dusk" as an opener, but never says the forest has no dusk (the v0.11.241 lesson: a text must agree with the place's first description).
Suggested (checked: 0 tics, 0 runs, 0 self, 0 time words): 10 "From the rise the dark seems to come up, not down. … and the sky is what goes under at the end. … as if after dark it ran over different stones."; 11 "Before the dark drops you have stepped over…"; 12 "Quiet has a sound here, and it is the water. The dark is slow to finish, for once, and the black trunks go from shapes to guesses. …"; 14 "Coming up from the water as the dark drops, …"; 15 "Mist lies along the stream when the dark has come, level as milk in a dish, …".
