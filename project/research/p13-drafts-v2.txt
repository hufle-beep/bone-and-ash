# notes
- 6:4 assumes white is this world's mourning colour and that James still wears the white lab coat about Ashfall; the jacket "kept in cedar" is the father's by implication only, never said. "Near the post" names no day.
- 6:5 assumes James's mother is alive on his world (his people live 800-900 years) and that Yuki, born the same day, has met her. If canon holds otherwise, "mother" can become "family" with small changes. Yuki is in one sentence that names no form.
- 6:6 takes the red tassel off Mei Lin's ivory hairpin for this one meal. If later scenes (the roof, the tribute) describe the tassel on the pin, either the cat/great passage's ending (the tassel on Yuki) or a returned tassel needs squaring; simplest fix is to cut {yuki} and let the tassel stay on the table.
- Word counts with the longest Yuki passage: 149, 147, 148.
- 6:6's three Yuki passages share their opening clause by design (one draft's alternatives, not separate drafts).
- "the honoured one" follows the given texts' usage for the woman form.
