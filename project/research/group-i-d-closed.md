## 3. Considered and skipped

- **Need a queue:** Increlution's queue and "right-click to queue single gathers"; Loop For A While's Route Panel and queue sim; Space Loop Protocol's queue and Auto-Repeat; Your Chronicle's templates (Selene's preset rows); Echo Station's planned route.
- **Already done in Bone and Ash:** familiarity tiers with names (`FAM`); shared familiarity across a group (Terraformental v0.4.0; ours: a quarter to the group's other cards, `tasks.js`); a one-click win when far stronger (BIOTOMATA's Decimate; ours: "End it in one move", `story.js`); what carries over and what resets shown plainly (Echo Station's page; our Coil screen); a single helper doing a chore (Your Chronicle's Sloth; ours: Yuki's lane); locked cards with a reason (Terraformental v0.3.0; our "opens when…"); a Journal that keeps everything (Space Loop Protocol's 15-entry log complaint; ours keeps it across Coils); text folded on a repeat (Terraformental praised "nothing to skip"; ours `again.js`).
- **Doesn't fit an offline story game with no timer:** offline time, nitrogen / NITRO (Space Loop Protocol); speed past the cap in real time (Terraformental's soft cap); a time-in-run multiplier cap (BIOTOMATA); clickers and auto-clicker limits (The Aetherium Loop); leaderboards, server events, daily missions (Chronicles of Cultivation, The Reincarnation System).
- **Clones replaying your past loops** (Loop Forge): that is a recorded queue, and Selene skipped "a ghost of the previous seedling". It doesn't fit "threads never make story choices" either.
- **ITRTG challenges** (repeatable, a bonus each time): Selene didn't pick them; maybe after the retune, as Coil-count challenges ("Act 1 in 2 Coils"). The personal best (fewest Coils) already covers the main measure.
- **Increlution's chapter reordering and funnel toggles per run:** our acts are a fixed story.
- **Karma shapes the next body** (Four Heavenly Valleys): our reset keeps who James is; it is a different loop.
- **Auto battles and summoned teams** (Your Chronicle, Sky Realm Idle): our fights are chosen round by round, and the story depends on it.
- **Space Loop Protocol's two exits per chapter:** our branches already do this (four Act 1 endings).
- **Universal Reconstruction, The Mana Tree:** prestige trees with no loop story; nothing for us.

## 4. Avoid list (seen there)

- Automation that fails silently or runs on: Increlution players' food cooking everything, with workarounds in three settings (Steam thread). A thread that idles or drops a tier must say so in Results.
- Unlock thresholds that take dozens of runs when each try is harder (Increlution funnel thread: 10 completions). Practised (familiarity 2) is fine; don't raise it.
- Automation walking into danger: an automated shield followed by a fight at low HP (Increlution). Threads stop before door cards and fights.
- Settings that reset every loop (Terraformental had to add a switch); boss automation lost on prestige, which players disliked (Journey to Ascension).
- Auto-resolve firing too early (BIOTOMATA's auto-decimate): our one-move must stay at "crushing" only.
- Hazards with too little feedback (The Reincarnation System's bolts; Terraformental's explosion distance). A tell or a deadline must be readable.
- Too many attention markers (Chrono Bot's "!"), and a short log that loses story text (Space Loop Protocol, 15 entries).
- Punishing hidden stats (Xianxia Simulator's Mental State, since reworked) and tooltips that disagree with the effect (Infinite Dao's lifespan).
- Text that doesn't follow the world state: Terraformental's dam logs ignored arriving after it reopened, and needed "message variations" (v0.3.0 comments). Our story tests should walk a scene in each state it can be reached in.
- Flavour text that doesn't progress: a Space Loop Protocol commenter on its AI-written text. Keep the rewrite pass's comparison honest.
- Tedious opening steps every loop (Terraformental comments, "early game tedious"; Selene's list).
