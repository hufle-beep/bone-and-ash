supper:5:0 | {OPEN} Her mother no longer watches you as she would a fox in the henhouse. She watches you as she would a fox she might find a use for. ¶ Tonight she talks. Of Mei Lin's father, who had a spirit root, and who went up the road with the collectors when Mei Lin was six. Of the letter that came down four years later, two words long: *Tribulation. Honoured.* Of the box they buried, which had nothing in it. ¶ Mei Lin clears the bowls through all of it, as loudly as bowls can be cleared. When the telling is done she says, with her back to the room, "He'd have liked you. He was an idiot too." ¶ "*Mei Lin.*" The widow says it once, and it closes like a door. "You do not speak so of your father." Mei Lin does not apologise. She does not stop smiling either, and she does not look at her mother again until the meal is over.
# notes
"her mother" now twice (first and last paragraphs); the two middle uses became "When the telling is done" (which also makes "she" plainly Mei Lin) and "The widow says it once"; "the mother" not used.
