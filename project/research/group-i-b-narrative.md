## 3. Considered and skipped

- **Real-time pieces (no timer since v0.11.64):** A Dark Room's cooldown buttons and enemy attack timers as such (#10 keeps only the idea as rounds), its 3–6 minute event clock, Evolve's falling event timer (a pity timer), Level 13's real-time fights and minimum repeat minutes, Kittens' "observe" window (Bone and Ash already gates cards by hour and day: `fo_spy`).
- **Items carried across a Coil:** A Dark Room's cache (`Prestige.collectStores`: a fraction of the last game's stores in "a destroyed village"). The design resets the hammerspace; REMEMBER already carries knowledge of places.
- **Purchased now, applied at the reset** (Bitburner's augmentations): a second currency of "pending" upgrades; the Insights and Coil marks cover permanent gains. Maybe later with Insights, not now.
- **Faction enemies / lock-outs** (Bitburner `enemies`): the romance choice (group f) already covers the one exclusive choice; more lock-outs would fight the "every branch reachable across Coils" design.
- **Standing rows for factions:** already on the roadmap (research.md #39, section 4); Kittens' "standing changes deal outcomes" is folded into #8.
- **Random rolls on outcomes** (A Dark Room's `nextScene` probabilities and loot chances, Evolve's random event effects, Candy Box 2's P potion): Bone and Ash is deterministic (no dice since v0.9.3; `d6()` is the save's seeded randomness for story events). #7 uses the seed, never a reroll.
- **Spatial play:** A Dark Room's world map, distance tiers and outfitting by weight; Candy Box 2's ASCII quests, cave patterns and cauldron timing; Level 13's sectors. They don't fit a story-stop game. (Outfitting before the Act 1 road was weighed: low value against the hammerspace caps already there.)
- **Already done or covered:** progressive reveal (P0), hints and next-step lines (Selene's picks, v0.11.72), the save-key registry and parity tests (Selene's rounds), merged log lines, splash texts (Space Company: a news-ticker cousin, on Selene's avoid list), CivClicker's morale (the Village relationship already moves in scenes).
- **Nothing for this cluster's focus:** Swarm Simulator, Sandcastle Builder, Space Company (beyond what Selene took), CivClicker (beyond the above).

## 4. Avoid list (seen there)

- **One random line per event with no memory** (Evolve `Math.rand` picks, Level 13's `Math.random` dialogue picks, Shark Game's `SharkGame.choose`): repeats are guaranteed. Bone and Ash's "never repeat within a Coil" needs a played list, not a die.
- **Hash-picked flavour without a played list** (the same failure, and ours today in `again.js`).
- **Hidden failure-earned rewards with no line** (A Dark Room tells you only in a passing notification): every earned thing should say so on screen and on the Coil screen.
- **Status effects that only show as a CSS class or colour** (A Dark Room's `fighter.attr('class', status)`): Bone and Ash's telegraphs must be in the text, for the phone and for screen readers.
- **Content flags that break the tone:** Evolve's slave events, CivClicker's zombies and corpse-eating as jokes; Bone and Ash's humour is deadpan, not grim-for-laughs.
- **Huge data files with no tests** (Level 13's 385 KB `DialogueData.json` needed its own data tests to stay whole): any pool system gets its test on day one.
- **Odds as percentages to the player** (Level 13 shows % in debug only; keep it so: numbers stay behind Show checks).
- **Pity timers and falling event clocks:** they make events feel random and untestable; the bots need the seed.
