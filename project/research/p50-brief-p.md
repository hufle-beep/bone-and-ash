That screen ends: the whole village out, faces up; a grandmother praying with her forehead in the dust; hunters gripping spears; everyone with any sense for Qi reaching for James and finding nothing, a mortal; Old Wen in his doorway, pipe gone cold, saying quietly that a tribulation withdrew its own verdict; someone running up the road to the Crimson Lotus; "Ashfall watches it go in total silence.
