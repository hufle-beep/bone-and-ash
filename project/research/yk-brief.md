## Background

The Coil: when an act's loop ends, James wakes again at the act's first dawn. James remembers every Coil. **Yuki remembers too** (she is bound to him; "It isn't as if either of us is allowed to forget"). Everyone else (Mei Lin, Ya, the villagers) starts over and remembers nothing.

Yuki's realm starts over with James's each Coil, so her human shape comes back only when he breaks through to Nascent Soul again. The first time it ever happened she asked him "family or more?" (the texts in the packets and `now.md`). Until now the answer stuck for good and she never asked again. **From now on she asks again every Coil, the first time she has a human shape in that Coil**, right after his Nascent Soul breakthrough ("Get up." leads straight to her). The owner's brief: **she remembers, is patient, doesn't push.** His answer can be the same as last time or different; "More." is offered only at high affection, "Not yet" only below it.
