## Background

At the village's communal pot, once the village trusts him fully, James can cook with the women. Today the card shows once a Coil in each act, and its scene is the one in the packet (the corpse-ears in the prologue, the noodles in the Fallout). **From this push, on the long calendar, it comes back every 3rd day of the stay (about 7 times a stay).** The first cook of each Coil keeps today's scene. **Each later cook shows one of 3 new repeat scenes per act**, never the same one twice in a row. The repeats give only the meal (no friendship gain), so they must not read as a turning point: the women already accept him; this is a regular shift at the pot.

**What the repeats can assume:** the first cook of this Coil happened (the corpse-ears, "the stranger's mushroom"; or the noodles), so the women know him at the pot. Anything else may or may not have happened, in any order: any repeat can be the 2nd cook or the 7th, on any day of the stay. **The Coil:** everyone but James and Yuki forgets at the end of an act's loop; within one Coil they remember.

**When they show:** the card shows 09:00–14:59 and the scene runs an hour, so it is the day's main meal at the pot, between late morning and mid-afternoon. **No time words at all** (morning, noon, midday, afternoon, evening, dawn, sun overhead, today, tomorrow, yesterday, "this time of day"), and no count of days or visits ("third time", "every few days", "again" is fine once).

**Prologue (`pot_cook`, 3 repeats):** Ashfall Hamlet, poor, the Crimson Lotus tribute coming (don't name how far off). Six or so women at the pot, the eldest who renamed the corpse-ears, girls sent on errands. **Mei Lin's mother is not at this pot** (do not put her in these three). Food to hand: barley, beans, millet, greens, ginger, dried fish, pork fat, salt, eggs, the corpse-ears, wild garlic; little else.

**Fallout (`fo_pot_cook`, 3 repeats):** the same village in Act 1, building a bone palisade against an Elder's coming: builders and hunters to feed, flour short, the women stretching it. **Mei Lin's mother is at this pot** (stern, 60); she can be in one or two of the three, not all. Food: as above, plus flour, noodles drying on a line.

**Avoid repeating the existing jokes:** the corpse-ears' poison and the noodles' first making (already told); dödölle "minus the planet" and shakshuka "with everything removed" (Mei Lin's mother's kitchen uses them). Pick other dishes from his list or plain village food done his way (e.g. dumplings, a pesto made of wild garlic, spenótfőzelék with eggs, French toast with no bread worth the name, a soup, flatbread on a hot stone); each repeat a different dish and a different small beat (an exchange, a correction, a child, a builder, a woman testing him). Music: none in these (it has its own lines elsewhere).

## The texts (6 sets, each: label, text, choice, result)

- **label**: the card on the hub, an imperative, at most about 8 words, ending with a full stop (today: "Join the women at the village pot." / "Help the women feed the builders."). Each repeat its own, clearly a regular shift, not the first meeting.
- **text**: 2 short paragraphs, about 90–150 words in all (shorter than the first-time scenes: it is seen again and again).
- **choice**: the one button, 2–7 words, in James's voice or plain (today: "Stay alive. Have a bowl." / "Take a bowl before the builders do.").
- **result**: a noun phrase the game ends with ": +25 satiety." (today: "Noodles in fish broth, eaten standing among the builders, who hold out their bowls a second time and offer no opinion"); under about 25 words; no full stop at the end.
