### W-sister
Yuki lets out a breath she has plainly been rationing.

"So that's what was waiting behind 'not yet'," she says. "Worth the queue."

"You're not disappointed."

"I put a question with two right answers, James. You picked one of them." She comes closer and butts her forehead against your shoulder, hard, twice. "I'll want it in writing eventually. Your handwriting is a disgrace, so I'm prepared to be flexible about when." Her tail curls round your wrist and holds on. "Sister. I'm keeping that. You'll find I'm very difficult to return."

### W-lover
Your hand is barely out before hers is in it, prompt, as though the offer might be withdrawn on a technicality.

"So 'not yet' did have an end," Yuki says. "I'd begun to think of it as furniture."

"It took me one whole turn of the world."

"I've seen you take longer over a menu. I'm encouraged." Her ears have gone forward, and her voice hasn't quite kept its edge. The kiss she gives you is short and entirely sure of its address. Then she tips her weight against your side, her grip on your fingers tightening. "Everything else can wait its turn. I've had the practice."

### N-again
Yuki tilts her head, ears following a beat behind. "Not yet it is," she says, no heavier than if you'd declined tea. "I'll put it to you again next time round. It's a decent question. It bears repeating, and so, apparently, do we." Her tail flicks once, untroubled.
