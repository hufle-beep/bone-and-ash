Both scenes are written as if James were alone; measured, the women are there in most Act 1 cases (Random bots 94 of 109 Act 1 tiger scenes; the Cultivator 154 of 154; the Sitter's human scene 102 in 100 games).

**Where the paragraph goes (Claude inserts it; you write only the paragraph):**

(In `yuki_human_after` she says she will choose who hears her answer back: leave room for that; the women do not get an answer from her in your paragraph.)
