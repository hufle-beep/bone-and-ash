## aw1

Ashfall, unfortunately, was looking. Every door on the street is open and has somebody standing in it, and Pock-Face stands with his head bowed and his hands folded, though nobody has died. The old women were counting the strokes, as is proper. They got as far as none.

Old Wen walks out to the place where the bolt gave up. He studies it, then you, with the good eye and then the milky one, in case either is lying.

"Heaven came for this man and found no charge to bring," he tells the street, in the voice kept for boundary disputes. "The Elder will ask what Ashfall saw. We will say that, and nothing more. Let no one improve on it."

Nobody improves on it. A hunter bows, gravely, like a man signing for a delivery he did not order. You nod back. It is cheaper than explaining.

## Notes

- `aw1` is about 150 words, the top of the range. The last paragraph can go whole if it must be shorter; the block then ends on Old Wen's "Let no one improve on it."
- `aw1`, "The Elder will ask what Ashfall saw": true while Shen Qiu is coming. If James can still break through in `rt_ashfall` on Day 21 after the Elder has arrived, the line is wrong there; swap it for "The Sect will ask what Ashfall saw."
- `aw1` names no time, tribute ending or palisade. "Every door … is open" holds at midnight too (people came out for the storm). I kept away from the prologue's "can't feel you" beat on purpose.
- `hf`: "climb", not fly, because the realm 1 breakthrough comes before he can fly. I assumed the dead Tree keeps a crown with boughs; the packet shows only the cracks. If it has none, alternative: "You climb the widest crack to where it opens on the sky, and find a level place on the lip."
- `hf` promises no sky, so realms 10 and 11 read true after it.
- `hf:ground`: "fizzles out in the dead bark". Alternative if "in" jars: "wood dust".
