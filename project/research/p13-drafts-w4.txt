supper:6:4 | The lamp has been trimmed to a bead, and the shutters were barred before the pot left the fire. Nobody explains it. You find your bowl by its warmth and the greens by guesswork. ¶ "A house that shows a light has oil to spare," Mei Lin's mother says, serving by touch and spilling nothing. "A house with oil to spare can spare other things. It is noticed from the road." ¶ "So we're poor by arrangement," says Mei Lin, somewhere on your left. "Mind the fish bones. If you choke, choke modestly." ¶ "We are not poor. We are not conspicuous." A dish is set down, exactly. "Your grandmother ate so, in this season, and hers before. Nobody choked." ¶ "Nobody was heard to." ¶ Her mother lets that pass in the dark, where it cannot be seen to have been allowed. Yuki, who alone can see her supper, eats it with unkind ease.
# notes
- Moment: the house eats by a low lamp behind barred shutters while the banners hang, so the road sees no oil to spare. The Sect and the collectors are never named.
- Yuki is in one form-neutral sentence (no {yuki} keys); "eats" and "see" suit all three forms.
- No coat, clothes, mourning or husband; none of the five other week-three moments or the given texts' moments. "Fire" in paragraph 1 is the cooking fire only; say if it reads too near week two and I'd swap it for "left the hearth".
- "in this season" is the only time phrase; no clock words, no day counts.
