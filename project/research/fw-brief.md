## The question

From Foundation (realm 1, his first breakthrough) James flies. The FAQ: "His people gain flight with their first breakthrough: they ride the Qi flows the way a swimmer rides a river's current, and to him it is as ordinary as walking. Yuki flies too, in every form. Since the realm starts over with each Coil, flight comes back with that Coil's first breakthrough." A walk to another place takes its time ÷ (1 + realm). Canon card: locals fly only from Golden Core, so nobody in the prologue flies but him and Yuki; **never in the prologue's fights** (fights stay on the ground, by design).

Bot games (task mode): 48 of 200 Random games, 163 of 200 Cultivator games and 100 of 100 Sitter games reach Foundation in the prologue; after that they visit these 61 scenes (table below). Every text in your packet can show while James can fly, and most can also show before he can (the same text). Only the Wilds hub has flight branches today.

Find every line (scene text paragraph ¶n, choice label [n], result line, the next screen's opening) that **reads wrong when James can fly**. Classes:

- **B (breaks):** the line says or assumes he can't fly or must go on foot: a climb, wade, crossing or long walk presented as the only way; being stuck, cut off, carried, slowed by ground; a travel time or distance that flight would erase; "you'd never make it in time" and the like; anything another character says that assumes he walks.
- **O (odd):** walking reads as a choice nobody remarks on where a flyer would plainly fly (alone, a long way, in a hurry), or flight would obviously change the scene and nothing acknowledges it (a chase, a lookout, a rescue, a scouting trip).
- **R (realm-blind, side list):** the line ignores that he has cultivation now (e.g. someone reads him as a mortal, "no cultivation at all", he has no Qi) — not flight, but the same state. List these separately.

**Not a break:** walking beside someone (Mei Lin, Ya, the villagers) at their pace; short steps inside a room, a street, a camp; fights on the ground (canon); a line that only shows before his first breakthrough (use the packet's "when (C2)" lines and the scene's own wording; if a line can only come before Foundation, say so and skip it); Yuki's own movement.

Be exact: quote at most the few words that break (under 15 words), name scene and ¶ / label index. Say how sure you are when the packet doesn't tell you whether the line can show at Foundation+.
