talk_mei:3:plain | She gives them back at arm's length, held by one earpiece, as one returns a beetle to its owner. Then she watches you put them on, sees your eyes change size, and laughs until the basket has to be steadied with her free hand.
talk_mei:4:plain | You try the money depth on her, as near as you can judge it. She laughs outright, names a sum, and keeps her palm held out until you have searched every pocket of the coat.
talk_mei:5:plain | You say you could carry her. She laughs hard enough to spoil the limp she isn't having, and gives you the basket instead, as the half of her worth carrying.
talk_ya:5:like | She stops you with a hand flat on your chest, sniffs your neck from collar to ear, and bites the ear, gently for her. Not food, she decides; food does not stand still for it. She stays near enough to bite again, in case she is wrong.
talk_ya:3 | Ya has taken against your coat, and has decided the coat ought to hear about it.
talk_mei:4:away:last | She looks at the haze, where somebody is no doubt bowing to nothing at all and doing it badly, and says the lesson goes better with a worse pupil in it.
# notes
- The three Mei Lin plain branches end on an act or a remark of hers (steadying the basket; her hand out for the money; handing him the basket). None looks round, so none clashes with "she laughs without looking behind her first".
- talk_mei:4:plain and 5:plain reword the openers ("You bow to her at the money depth." / "You offer to carry her.") to keep clear of the old five-word runs; restore the old openers if the matter should read identically.
- talk_ya:5:like: a hand on his chest and a bite on the ear, no arm. Text 4's warm branch also ends at his ear (the blush); swap "the ear" for "your shoulder, through the coat" if two ears in the cycle is one too many.
- talk_mei:4:away:last keeps "haze" on purpose (this version shows in the Ashen Hills only) and has someone in it, unnamed.
