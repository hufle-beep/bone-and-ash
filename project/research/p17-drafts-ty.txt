talk_ya:11 | Ya has gone quiet over a slab of black glass. She crouches in the ash with it tilted to the sky, and the face in it looks back at her, scar and all, sharper than any pool ever gave it. ¶ She does not speak. She bares her teeth at it, and it bares them back. She touches the scar under her eye and watches the other hand do the same on the wrong side. You wait. Nobody has asked you to, and with Ya that is as near to an invitation as it gets. ¶ When she stands, a splinter of the glass comes with her, wrapped in a scrap of hide.
talk_ya:11:warm | She holds the splinter up so that both your faces are in it, yours above hers, and studies the pair. "Good," she decides. "Better lying down." She tucks it away where, she says, you are welcome to go looking.
talk_ya:11:cold | She turns the splinter on you and looks from the face in it to yours and back. "The glass one is better," she says. "It keeps its mouth shut." She walks on, pleased with both of them.
