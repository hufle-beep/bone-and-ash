# notes
- vm:3:x shared texts place James at the door, the table and the shutters of "Mei Lin's house" and never name the loft, so they hold after a burn; vm:3:4:burned assumes the loft has shutters (if not, swap in vm:3:2's or vm:3:3's pattern).
- vm:3:2 Yuki: "forty years" rests on her being born the same day as James (canon card); it is an age, not a day count.
- vm:3:3 assumes breakfast is eaten at the mother's table with Mei Lin there; "the mother" in the human key and :burned follows the supper text's usage.
- vm:1:2 assumes Mei Lin hangs the loft's herbs herself (wormwood is my pick of herb) and that the loft has rafters and a hatch; vm:1:3 assumes the great form shares the loft.
- far_won:5 assumes the hunters butcher on the spot and that offering the opening cut reads as an honour; it names no place, so it does not lean on the swept ground.
- supper_w3:5: the first "her mother" is kept; the second and the three Yuki openings became "the mother". The human key now has "the mother" three times; "Mei Lin's mother rises" is the alternative for its opening.
- roof_stay:3:2:burned uses "the loft and its charred beams" to vary from the other two; "the scorched loft" fits as well.
