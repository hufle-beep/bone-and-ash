## The problem
In week 2 the slopes show one of two texts, in turn. Each ends with Mei Lin's question about why James is up there, and the choice under it reads "Answer her question. Honestly." But when her lip is split, a line about the lip is added **after** the question, so the screen ends on the lip and the choice reads as if it answers the lip. The lip lines must move **before** the question, between the text's first paragraph and her question's paragraph, and be rewritten to fit there.

Why her lip is split: only in games where James, on the prologue's first day, saw from the ridge and did not come down to help; Gao Feng (the Sect's man) hit her on the herb slope. In the village he may or may not have gone to apologise; the lines must work either way. Week 2 is days later; she is over it on the surface, and it is not mentioned aloud. The two lines show healing in order: text 1 a scab, text 2 a thin pale line (keep that order; text 2 is always read after text 1).
