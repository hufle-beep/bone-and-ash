# PL drafts (v0.11.189; Fable, run 1, about 78k tokens; word for word)

### PA
"Which rung?" Mei Lin asks. "Core Formation," you say. She laughs until she has to hold her knees. "The Sect's elders are Core Formation. They spent centuries on it. They get carried places." Ya eyes the clearing sky. "Can he beat an elder?" "Ask an elder," you say.

### PB
"True Core," you report, since neither of them can feel it. Mei Lin sits down where she is and laughs at the sky. "Elders die waiting for that one. Old, and waiting." Ya lowers her spear, disappointed in the lightning. "It patted you down and left. I wanted to watch it try."

### PC
Mei Lin laughs until the sky has finished leaving. Then she doesn't. "Sect masters stand about where you are sitting. It costs them lifetimes, and there are banners." Ya grins. "And they float up like old smoke. You go up like a thrown rock, scholar." "Thank you," you say.

### PD
"Say the name," Mei Lin says. You say it. She nods slowly. "That one is in the stories the old women tell. The man in it has a beard to his knees and a mountain." Ya looks you over. "You have glasses and a coat. Who would you lose to?" "Paperwork," you say.

### PE
You are still looking up when Mei Lin clears her throat. "Was that all of it? You sat. Nothing came." "It has stopped coming," you say. She starts to laugh and it turns into a stare. Ya spits to one side. "Then it is afraid of you. Good. I never liked it."

### PF
Mei Lin has watched all of it with her arms folded. "Immortal," she says, trying the word on you. "I should bow. I can't. I have watched you apologise to a mushroom." Her voice gives a little at the end. Ya plants her spear. "Fight me anyway, scholar. I want to say I lost to one."

### MA
Mei Lin prods your shoulder with one finger, checking it for heat. "Core Formation?" You nod. "The Sect's elders are Core Formation. They are older than my grandmother's grandmother, and we kneel to them." She laughs, short and unsteady. "And Ya chose this to sulk through." "I can describe it," you say. "It is mostly sitting."

### MB
"True Core," you tell her. Mei Lin counts it off on her fingers, mortal, disciple, elder, and runs out of people she has met. "I have no one to compare you to," she says, and laughs, and then looks at you as if that were not funny. "Ya would. She would say herself."

### MC
Mei Lin laughs first. Then she walks a slow circle round you, as she would round a herb she can't name. "A sect master stands about where you are. I would not be let within a field of one." She does not step back. "Ya took her spear and her temper and missed it. I am going to enjoy telling her."

### MD
"What is this one called?" Mei Lin asks. You tell her. She laughs through her nose. "Nobody has that. That is carved over temple doors, with a beard." Then, quieter: "People would walk a month to see you sit there. Ya walked the other way." "She has a good sense of direction," you say.

### ME
You wait. Mei Lin waits with you, her arm half raised. Nothing arrives. "Is it late?" she says. "It isn't coming." She lowers the arm and laughs at herself, then frowns upward. "It is supposed to come. It comes for everyone." "I think we've stopped speaking," you say.

### MF
Mei Lin lets out a breath she seems to have kept through the whole of it. "Immortal," she says. "There should be a mountain under you, and a thousand people on their faces." She looks round the camp. "There's me." She is smiling, and she means it. "And Ya will come back wanting to fight one."

### TA
Mei Lin's head comes up through the crack, braced for a corpse. She finds you sitting, and a clear sky. "The storm was over this tree," she says. "I ran." Ya climbs past her, sniffs the air and scowls. "No burnt meat. Which rung, scholar?" "Core Formation." Mei Lin laughs so hard she nearly loses the ledge.

### TB
They come up the crack arguing about who should look first. Ya wins, looks, and is annoyed. "Not a mark. Heaven went through you like a thief through an empty house." Mei Lin sits on the dead bark and gets her breath. "True Core," you offer. She points at you. "Don't. Let me climb down before I understand that."

### TC
Ya arrives first, spear in her teeth, and takes it out to talk. "Sect masters do this on a peak, with a crowd. You did it in a dead tree." Mei Lin, behind her, starts laughing, and then looks up at the sky for some while without any. "It had a vacancy," you say.

### TD
Mei Lin climbs up, finds you whole, and does not bother to look surprised. "Name?" You give it. "That is a realm from the old stories," she says, and her voice is not quite steady. "They end with the man leaving the world." Ya's head appears at her elbow. "He can't. He owes me a hunt."

### TE
The two of them come up the crack because nothing has happened and it has gone on too long. Mei Lin looks at the unmarked dead bark, then the sky. "No storm?" "It didn't come." Ya laughs once, loudly, inside the hollow trunk. "You scared off Heaven, scholar. What is left for me to hunt?" "Smaller things," you say.

### TF
Ya comes up the crack with her spear ready, sees there is nothing to stab, and takes it personally. Mei Lin follows and settles on the dead bark across from you. "Immortal," she says. "In a hollow tree, in that coat." She isn't laughing. "Should we kneel?" "Please don't," you say. "I'd have to get up."
