# notes
- rv:s54: the custom is now a neighbourly offer of help with the fence; "It took too long" still fits, and the mother's next line answers Mei Lin's "Let me tell him."
- rv:w54a/w54b: the closeness is her walking in the furrow to keep abreast; she does not laugh, and the text ends on her line with the tribute's shadow.
- rv:w56b ends on James's line (w54 no longer does); she holds a straight face, no laugh and no look up the lane.
- rv:w65 keeps "I have always thought he overdoes it" and names the collectors; "her arm through yours" may echo an old third-week walk, worth a script check.
- rv:s44: the penalty is tied to the dead she joked about; the last sentence keeps its old ending, with "her mouth" in place of the stray "it".
