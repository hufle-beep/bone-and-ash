### Chinese design writing read

- **GameRes, 一念逍遥's lead designer's retrospective** (and a second GameRes interview): cultivation (修仙) is about personal growth through breakthroughs, not chivalry; a higher realm must *decisively* beat a lower one, so each realm is a multiple of the last; they rejected turn-based and MUD prototypes because they did not *feel* like cultivation; novels give companions about a fifth to a third of the hero's power; the hero ends up friendless because no one keeps pace. Warning in their own words' sense: exponential numbers forced them to keep opening new servers.
- **gcores, "From Tale of Immortal: why do we like cultivation games?"**: a limited lifespan creates urgency; each breakthrough lifts the cap and feels like shedding a mortal limit; breakthroughs as earned promotions; chance encounters (机缘) feel like fate; relationships (masters, Dao companions, grief) pull harder than power; tiresome: the "刷刷刷" farming loop that drowns the story, too many systems at once, demands so high players take a lesser route.
- **gcores, "Why Tale of Immortal's journey fascinates me"**: breakthroughs split into smaller goals (experience plus specific materials); the late game rises only by numbers, so monsters stop being new; tasks are generic errands.
- **China Writers (chinawriter.com.cn) on Wangyu's 凡人修仙传** (the founding "mortal-flow" novel): a strict ladder where the strong treat the weak as nothing and may kill over a slight; scarcity, caution, solitary retreats; longevity as the core motive; the formula's flaw is repetitive level-up structure.
- **China Writers on 季越人 (玄鉴仙族, a family cultivation novel)**: realism as low cultivators surviving the schemes of high ones; fragile, expendable lives; characters killed at their peak.
- **Huxiu, "In the name of the Dao"**: realm ladders read as social hierarchy and exam-style mobility; readers enjoy the looked-down-on hero proving everyone wrong; "hidden elder" heroes make established powers look foolish through information gaps.
- **Sohu (a Zhihu column's mirror), how to build 扮猪吃虎 ("play the pig to eat the tiger")**: four parts (a truly strong hero, a credible reason for hiding, secrecy, a key moment with a payoff); the disguise stretch matters more than the reveal: it gives many small pleasures, the reveal one short peak; witnesses are essential, the crowd's shock is half the payoff; the antagonist's contempt should be exaggerated; a calm understated hero makes him look foolish; failures: no motive, humiliation so harsh readers quit, a reveal at an unreasonable moment, no audience, a hero obviously strong too early.
- **Gamersky on the "kill the whole family" trope**: the payoff cliché of industrial web novels and why it went stale (not for us: James never kills humans).

## 3. Considered and skipped

- **Breakthrough odds, pity counters, breakthrough pills as hard gates** (xiantu, 想不想修真, 修仙家族模拟器): no dice since v0.9.3, and James needs no herbs (lore). The local's breakthrough (I4) can *describe* pills and odds.
- **A tribulation mechanic for James** (xiantu's three rounds): James gets none by the owner's spec; used only as a text shape for I4.
- **Heart demons as a mechanic** (心魔): nothing in the lore gives James one; a heart-demon text for a local fits I4. A "Coil heart demon" (a breakthrough replaying the last collapse) was considered and left out: it bends "no tribulation" and is the owner's call if wanted.
- **Lifespan as a clock** (寿元): our clocks are the act deadlines; lifespan stays a social fact (I1) and a motive in texts.
- **Seclusion as a time skip in normal Coils**: it would eat a deadline; the Long Forest already has it (`lf_seclusion`, "a season passes").
- **Offline and real-time progress** (xiantu's cave, 一念逍遥's always-online body, 想不想修真): a story game with task cards, no timer.
- **Families, generations, breeding, marriages as management** (修仙家族模拟器): one hero and his cat; Selene-style avoid (management chores).
- **Talent drafts, stat allocation, random fates per life** (lifeRestart, xiantu's 吉凶 fates, 轮回修仙's rerolls): James is fixed, and random rolls are out; xiantu's "every bad fate comes with a compensation" noted as a rule if anything random ever returns.
- **Choose one thing to carry into the next life** (lifeRestart's locked talent): our carry-overs are by rule, not by pick; gear stays lost (the owner's Loot pick; #94).
- **Leave something for your next self in the world** (lifeRestart's "the technique you left yourself"): the world resets; #94 territory.
- **Sect contribution, daily sect tasks, weekly sect wars, ranks with stipends, auction houses, markets** (xiantu, 一念逍遥, 不一样的修仙宗门2): treadmill economies (avoid list).
- **Alignment that is irreversible** (xiantu's demonic path): fights "every branch reachable across Coils".
- **LLM-driven NPCs** (cultivation-world-simulator): offline game, no model at runtime; its world-given nicknames are already ours in a fixed way ("Senior", "the Bone-Maker").
- **Exponential realm multipliers** (一念逍遥: each realm a multiple): ours is +0.3 per realm in `jamesPower`, with the felt gap carried by degrees and texts; exponential numbers are on Selene's avoid list.
- **Dual cultivation and possessive-partner plots** (某某宗女修): adult content; structure only (I1's attitude shift).
- **Gacha, lotteries, check-ins, dice minigames** (vue-XiuXianGame, react-xiuxian-game): nothing for us.
- **"Kill the whole family"** (Gamersky): James never kills humans.

## 4. Avoid list (seen there)

- Farming that drowns the story (刷刷刷): the gcores essays on Tale of Immortal, 修仙家族模拟器's late game, 不一样的修仙宗门2's month-skipping.
- Many opaque stats nobody can read (aptitude, comprehension, speed: 修仙家族模拟器's newcomers rely on outside guides).
- Automation that ignores the player's intent or piles everything on one unit (不一样的修仙宗门2).
- Random events that are only obstacles (修仙家族模拟器 reviews): an event should give a story or a choice, not just a loss.
- Wiping what was learned at rebirth (修仙立志传): crafts and recipes stay, as planned.
- No run summary; time that runs on after quitting (某某宗女修's complaints).
- A face-slap that repeats one shape, humiliation of the hero so harsh players quit, a hero obviously strong before the disguise has paid, a reveal with no audience (the 扮猪吃虎 essays).
- Realm flavour that lets locals *sense* James (it would undo the game's best running joke and the owner's 18:35 rule for Yuki too).
- Exponential power curves (一念逍遥 had to keep opening servers).
- Licence traps: README badges that contradict the licence file (vue-XiuXianGame), MIT claimed with no file (rxy), "reproduction" repos of commercial games (moyuwang-xiuxian).
