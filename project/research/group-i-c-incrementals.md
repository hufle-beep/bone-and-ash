## 4. Avoid list (seen there)

- Exponential multipliers (Gooboo sigils `1.4^lvl`, Trimps ×4 formations, ×5 toughness): our fights run on DC 2–9 and HP 3–30; every trait or stance must be ±0.5 / ±1 / ×1.5 at most.
- Chance-based combat effects (crit %, dodge %): deterministic patterns only (every Nth round, below half HP, after a tell).
- Achievement or feat power rewards (Trimps' % damage per achievement, AD's rewards): the retune would end up chasing them.
- Secret achievements that need out-of-game actions (AD: open the console, the Konami code, notation settings): keep secrets in-fiction.
- Mastery that resets every loop (Gooboo resets item mastery on prestige): if row 11 is built, keep it across Coils like familiarity.
- Hover-only tooltips for enemy and gear details (Gooboo, AD): a phone needs a tap or a visible line.
- Many overlapping currencies and tiers (Synergism fragments, Gooboo's dozens of currencies): one material per beast family, cores as money.
- Story only as log lines that scroll away (Trimps' Story messages mixed with loot): our story is scenes; one-liners go to the grouped Journal.
- Flashing or blended colour effects for story speakers (AD notes photosensitivity in `blendCel`).
- Date events, battle passes, premium currencies (Gooboo, Trimps' bones).
