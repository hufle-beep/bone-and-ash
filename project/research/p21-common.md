If James wins, three ways follow, chosen after the fight: send Lu Han home (the victory), make him believe a senior of a hidden sect beat him (the bluff), or pay the village's due anyway with a sung wraithbone hairpin (the pin). Lu Han now throws a spirit-beast net over Yuki in the first breath of every tribute fight (this was already canon in the lost fight's text), so she sits the fight out under it.
