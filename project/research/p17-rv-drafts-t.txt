tm:13 | Mei Lin has stopped beside the path over a low plant with grey leaves and a red stem, and she is frowning at it as at a stranger found in her kitchen. She knows every herb on the slope behind Ashfall by name and by use. This one has neither. ¶ You tell her that among your people whoever finds a thing gets to name it. She looks up, scandalised. "Names come down from the dead. Nobody *gives* one. Who would you be to?" The finder, you say. Usually they name it after themselves, in a language nobody speaks. ¶ "That is vanity twice over." She cuts a sprig, smells it, bites a leaf and spits. "Bitter. Stubborn. Grows where nothing asked it to." The sprig goes into the basket by itself, apart from the rest.
tm:13:warm | "James-wort," she says, trying the foreign shape of it with great seriousness, and does not smile until she has turned away. You notice the sprig has gone to the top of the basket, where nothing will crush it.
tm:13:cold | "It can wait for its name," she decides. "A name ought to be earned, and so far this one has only made my tongue numb." She walks on working her jaw, with considerable dignity.
ty:13 | "Your feet are blind, scholar," says Ya, looking down at your boots with real pity.
