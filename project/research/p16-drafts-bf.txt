# notes
- rr:4: assumes Mei Lin writes (she is village-schooled) and that James and Yuki can read the local script; Ya cannot read (canon). The name's shape is never described beyond "too many legs".
- rr:5: assumes James sees poorly without his glasses (canon gives him glasses, not how bad his eyes are).
- ah:4: assumes loose black glass can be dug up in panes and shows a reflection, and that stars are visible round the dead tree.
- ah:5: assumes ash falls from the sky in the Ashen Hills (canon says only ash and black glass); if it does not, the open, all four middles and "before the sky could season it" need a wind-blown reading instead. Assumes Ashfall uses coin and keeps goats.
- ah:4 `mei` and ah:5 `ya` use "your coat" (the lab coat).
- The `{yuki}` forms are worded apart on purpose, so no five-word run repeats between them.
