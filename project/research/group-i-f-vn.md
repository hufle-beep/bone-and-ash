## 3. Considered and skipped

- **An engine as the runtime** (inkjs, Yarn Spinner): both MIT, but adopting one means rewriting ~317 JS scenes and their tests; Yarn is C#; no build step allowed. The vocabulary (F2, F3) is the useful part.
- **Tracery** (Apache-2.0): grammar-generated lines read samey, fight the "no repeats" lint and the rule that Fable writes the text.
- **Twine extras:** `visited()`, `lastVisited()`, `memorize()` / `recall()` are what `g.seen`, `g.picked` and the kept flags already do; "days since" conditions fit inside F2's conditions.
- **Ren'Py's rollback, history and "skip unread too":** the Coil and the Journal cover the first two; skipping unread text defeats the fold's purpose. Ren'Py's `chosen` (a choice taken in any play) is already ours: the sepia picked mark (`pickedMark`, v0.11.32).
- **Jumping to any node** (VLR, AI, 13 Sentinels): it would bypass the Coil, its clock and its costs (F11 keeps the map, drops the jump).
- **Locals with flashes of déjà vu** (Higurashi's other worlds): against canon (`lore.md`: no one but James and Yuki remembers). The owner's call if wanted.
- **Steins;Gate's phone trigger** (choices made by replying or not): low for us; maybe later as Yuki's bond lines you can answer or let pass.
- **Umineko's red truth** (a text colour the game promises is true): a cute fit for Yuki's bond lines, but a colour-only signal is on round 1's avoid list; low.
- **Two protagonists / framed tales** (Ever17, The House in Fata Morgana, 428's five leads): one protagonist here; 428's lesson is in F9.
- **AI's six-minute timed investigations:** every card already costs game time.
- **Danganronpa's gift tiers and limited free-time slots:** the day clock already limits talks; gifts belong to the dating-sim cluster.
- **DDLC's poem words** (an avatar hops when she likes a word): live feedback on affection; low, and Results already shows relationship changes.
- **Deathloop's Residuum, Majora's songs:** keeping gear is #94; skipping time is what cards already do.

## 4. Avoid list (seen there)

- **Breaking the save or the device** (DDLC corrupts and deletes saves, makes the player delete files): against "saves are never orphaned"; and its horror content is out of tone.
- **Closing a finished route on later plays** (Little Busters!): every branch stays reachable across Coils.
- **Reactions that don't expire** (Hades, by players: congratulations several runs late, "as if it's the first time"): every reactive line gets a "still true" condition (F2).
- **Rote repetition** (Twelve Minutes: an hour re-clicking one talk to set up one new scene): any stretch read before must fold or skip.
- **A failure state from talking** (Elsinore's suspicion can end a run): in F4 a small standing cost only.
- **Overlong notes** (428: a couple of very long tips crashed the game): footnotes one line, tips short.
- **A hidden route meter with no feedback** (Katawa Shoujo shows nothing; the route arrives as a surprise): ours shows relationship changes in Results; keep it.
- **Adult VN content:** structure only (Katawa Shoujo, the dating VNs); romance fades to black at the door.
- **Conditions that change state** (Yarn's blog): a picker that writes while it chooses gives order-dependent, random-looking results.
