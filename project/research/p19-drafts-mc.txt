tb:mei30:camp | Mei Lin reaches over and takes the glasses off your face without asking. She breathes on each lens, polishes it on her sleeve, and holds the pair up to the fire to judge her work, in no hurry at all. Then she puts them back on you herself, with both hands, and her fingers stay at your temples after the job is done. Out of habit she glances round for a witness who is several days' walk behind her. "There," she says, from very near. "Now you can see what is in front of you." You can. You tell her the left one is still smudged.
tb:mei72:camp | Mei Lin has found a tear in your coat cuff and is mending it with you still inside the coat, which leaves your hand in her lap and nothing for you to do about it. She sews slowly, for a woman who is quick with a needle. Ya watches from the other side of the fire, chin on her spear shaft. "You make that seam long, herb girl." Mei Lin says some work should not be hurried, and does not look up. Ya grunts, scowls at the flames, and then holds out the frayed cord of her tooth necklace. "Mine after. Not so slow." Mei Lin takes it.
tb:ya72:tree | Ya walks to the edge of the firelight, plants her spear, and addresses the dark of the hall in the voice she keeps for wolves. The scholar, she informs it, sleeps inside her fire and eats from her kills, and is not to be taken by anything. Whatever wants him may come and discuss it with her. The hall keeps its own counsel. You remark that nobody is there. "Then nobody has heard it clearly," Ya says, and comes back to sit close against you, pleased with the evening's work. Mei Lin, from her watch, promises to inform any callers.
tb:mei30:camp:b | Mei Lin takes the waterskin out of your hand before you have stoppered it and drinks from it as it is. In Ashfall a girl wipes the neck with her sleeve before she drinks after a man, and makes sure she is seen doing it; Mei Lin explains this to you afterwards, pleasantly, as a point of local custom, with the skin not yet lowered. Her eyes stay on yours while she says it. Ya snorts. Mei Lin hands the skin back, neck towards you, and waits to see what a man of learning does with it. You drink. It seemed the scholarly course.
