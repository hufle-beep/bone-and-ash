Mei Lin wakes at the scrape of the pot and announces that she was resting her eyes for the ancestors' sake, who have seen enough of her. It comes out hoarse. - 9:7: assumes wild honey is in season and that the house owns a festival dish; "arse" is Ya's word, there to earn either choice ("Manners." or the laugh): "backside" if it is too strong.
- 9:7 has no Yuki sentence; the away line assumes serving by age is the house's habit.
