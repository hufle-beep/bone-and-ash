# FW brief (v0.11.195, owner pick F1): the prologue's texts for a James who can fly (Fable, one run)

Read `/tmp/fw/canon-card.md` and `/tmp/fw/packet.md` (the packets of every scene below). Read nothing else; do not open the game's files. Write drafts to `/tmp/fw/drafts.md` (format at the end); notes, if any, to `/tmp/fw/notes.md` (suggestions only, under 5 KB). Reply with one line when done.

**Tone (hard):** James and Yuki deadpan; locals overly serious (formal, grave about Heaven, face, the Sect; they don't joke); Mei Lin looser. Second person for James. Plain English. Avoid the canon card's tics (no "a good deal", "a great deal", "a long time", "for a moment", "the far side", etc.). Keep each draft as short as the line it replaces, or shorter where you can.

**Background.** In the prologue (James's first week in this world) James can break through to Foundation from Day 2; from Foundation he flies, as naturally as walking (his people ride the Qi currents like a swimmer rides a river; lore: "James is a natural"; he is unbothered by others' bafflement). The realm starts over every Coil, so flight comes back with each Coil's first breakthrough; his first breakthrough in the first Coil is also his first flight ever. **Locals can't sense his cultivation** (to their Qi-sense he is mortal; keep that); nobody else here flies. **No flight in the prologue's fights** (canon). A cold read found lines that read as if he can only walk. Each item below is a **variant shown only from Foundation** (the game picks it when he can fly) unless it says otherwise; the original stays for a mortal James. **Time:** the game's clock is fixed for work (digging, exploring, burying) and is not changing: a flown variant must not promise the job got shorter where the item says the time stays.

Where an item says "keep allowed", you may answer `KEEP` with one line why, instead of a draft.
