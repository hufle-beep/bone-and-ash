# notes
- mei6:open: keeps "in the basket, sorting by touch" from the old sentence on purpose (the brief asks to keep both); "minds her footing" holds on a root, a trunk, in water and on ash.
- mei9:ya: the spear stands in for the rock (she has it in all three places). She is barefoot, so the kick is at a shaft she is holding, not at anything hard on the ground. If three spear lines across the talks are too many, this is the one to keep.
- ya4:spear: "once for each of you" picks up her count in the sentence before (scholar, herb girl, cat) without a number. "as she goes" instead of "as she walks", since she may be wading.
- yaq:both:end: no place word at all; the sentence after ("Smaller. Rounder.") follows unchanged.
- yr:open: no "path", no "drags you aside", no "hauls"; "something large enough" is a trunk, a root or a slab of glass as the region has it. Kept the old ending "and waits for nothing".
- yaoff:2:4: 33 words. Assumes Mei Lin and her basket are in sight when the sulk plays; if the sulk can show with Mei Lin away, swap "the herb girl's basket" for "the teeth on her necklace" (then yr:open would share that image, so change one).
- ya9:both:kiss: 44 words. Uses neither "starts/begins" nor "ends": the red "runs down to" the collarbone, as at the spring, and the open question is "how far under" the cloth. Assumes Mei Lin has a root to hand (her basket).
- say:tagNowMany: 4 words, parallel to "the speech is now". A shorter spare: "speeches, now".
