supper:4:7 | Fish bones, in this house, go on the rim of the bowl and never on the table. You learn it by watching Mei Lin's mother look at yours. ¶ You move them, and being what you are, set them out in order, spine first, then the ribs by size. ¶ The mother studies the result. "It is not required to rebuild the fish," she says. ¶ "Ask him what it died of," says Mei Lin. ¶ "Smoke," you say. "Some days ago." ¶ "A guest is not asked to examine his supper." Her mother turns to you. "Tidy," she says, as a clerk notes a weight, and not yet in your favour. ¶ Mei Lin gathers the bowls, admiring yours aloud all the way to the basin.
supper:5:7 | Age is the question Mei Lin's mother has chosen for this meal: yours. Forty, you tell her. She sets down her bowl to weigh it. "And a wife?" ¶ "None." ¶ "Dead, or never?" ¶ "Never. I was at my studies." ¶ "For how long?" ¶ "Most of the forty. I passed shortly before I came here." ¶ "A slow scholar," she says, "or a hard examination." ¶ "Both." ¶ "So nobody would have him," says Mei Lin, pleased. ¶ "Mei Lin." ¶ "I am only asking what the lane will ask." ¶ "The lane does not eat here. Pour the guest's water, and be silent while you pour." ¶ Mei Lin fills your cup to the brim, slowly, with her eyes lowered, and you have rarely been told so much by a silence.

# notes
- Both suppers leave Yuki out (allowed by the brief), so they have no form keys.
- supper:4:7 assumes a house custom that fish bones go on the bowl's rim; invented, not in canon.
- supper:5:7 assumes James can say he studied "most of the forty" years and passed shortly before arriving (canon: 40, single, a new doctorate); the mother's question is the meal's one question, as week 2 has it.
- village_night:quiet:7 assumes the household goes to bed by rank, lodger first, and that the mother wears slippers indoors; the hairpin is Mei Lin's ivory one (canon), unnamed. "Climbs the ladder" holds for all three lead-ins (roof, dishes, early).
- The three Yuki passages share "I was not sent up at all" and "Draw your own conclusions" on purpose (one text by form), as the week 2 and 3 quiet texts do.
