### M1-N
Mei Lin has not moved. In the dark her face is turned up to where the storm was, then down to the scorched thumbprint the one bolt left in the ash by your knee. "I have watched Heaven burn three people," she says. "It never once changed its mind." A short laugh gets out of her. "Ya will call me a liar."

"Tell her it was a scheduling error," you say.

### M1-N alt
Mei Lin crouches in the dark and puts two fingers to the ash where the one bolt went out. Still warm. "I have carried home what Heaven leaves of people," she says. "It does not come all this way and go off empty-handed." Then she laughs, because the other choice is worse. "Ya sulked through this. She will not believe a word."

"It looked," you say. "I think I was a disappointment."

### M1-D
Mei Lin has not lowered the arm she threw over her eyes. She looks under it at the empty sky, then at you, who are not on fire. "People die of that," she says. "I have carried what was left of them home." Then she laughs, helplessly. "Ya walked off and missed Heaven losing its nerve. She will never believe me."

"I barely do," you say. "And I was closer."

### M1-D alt
Mei Lin walks a slow circle round the scorched patch in the ash, the only mark the whole storm left, and then round you. "I have seen that kill a man who had prepared for ten years," she says. "You did not even stand up." Her laugh comes out cracked. "Ya will say I made it up to spite her."

"Show her the patch," you say. "It's all the evidence I got."
