## How the texts come back (V3b)
On the long calendar (the game's default) these scenes come back on several evenings of one story day, and each slot has ONE text now. Each slot becomes a list of three, played in order one an evening, then round again: the old text stays first; you write the 2nd and 3rd. A text read before folds to its FIRST SENTENCE with a "Read it all again" link, so every text needs at least two sentences, and its first sentence must work alone as a reminder of the scene. The 2nd and 3rd texts are later evenings of the same stretch: the same situation, a different moment; they must not retell the old text's beat. No day counts, no "tomorrow", "last night", "first night" (which evening a text lands on varies).

Where a text names Yuki, give all three forms (`:cat`, `:great`, `:woman` variants of the whole text, sharing every sentence the form doesn't touch): the cat (small, on his shoulder; bond-speech in italics), the great root-kin (tiger-sized white cat; bond-speech), the woman (speaks aloud). In the prologue (the roof) Yuki is nearly always the cat; in Act 1 (the fireside) any form. Leaving her out is fine.
