## Part 1 — Yuki's human form with Mei Lin and Ya there (4 texts)

**Background.** In Act 1 Mei Lin and Ya travel with James. At realm 7 Yuki takes her human form for the first time (`now.md` H1); in most games the women watch it (their paragraph, P both / M Mei Lin alone with Ya away, is in H1) and hear her first words. Then `yuki_human_after` (packet), then straight into `yk_choice`, her private question: "What am I to you?" Both scenes were written as if James were alone.

- **HA-P, HA-M — a new ¶5 for `yuki_human_after`** (replaces its whole ¶5, "People say stupid things in front of cats … Promise you won't make an occasion of it."). The old ¶5 has her say she will "choose who hears me answer back": wrong now, the women have just heard her. Keep the idea that she will mostly stay a cat in front of people and pick whom she talks to, but it must take in that these two (P) / Mei Lin (M) have heard her already. It must still end on a request that the first choice, "Promise. No occasion.", answers, and the second, "Ask her to stay like this for a while.", still makes sense after it. Yuki speaks; one short beat from the woman or women at most (a look, a word); they don't get a long reply from Yuki. 40–80 words. ¶2 already says the great form "leaves everyone rather less room" when they are there.
- **HQ-P, HQ-M — an opening for `yk_choice`** (goes in front of its ¶1, only when the women saw her change). The women see that Yuki wants James alone and take themselves off for a while, so the question and its answers ("Family…", "More.", "Not yet.") are private. Mei Lin may tease on the way out; in P, Ya may need taking by the elbow. 25–50 words. **No shape words for Yuki** (after the first choice she is small again; after the second she stays human; ¶1 then puts her "in the tall shape"). **No time words** (night, dawn, sun, morning) and **no ground words** (moss, ash, dust, bark): it shows at camps, in the Old Tree, on the road.

## Part 2 — Yuki's great form in the prologue's Ashfall (1 text)

**Background.** In the prologue (James's first week; nobody here knows what he is), James can break through to Foundation in the village of Ashfall. The whole village comes out into the street to watch the tribulation that withdraws (`now.md` V1, the witness block: the kneeling grandmother, the hunters, Old Wen in his doorway). Its "Get up." leads straight to `yuki_tiger_awaken` (packet): his white cat grows to the size of a Bengal tiger, there in the street, and nobody reacts.

- **GV — a village paragraph for `yuki_tiger_awaken`**, inserted after ¶5 (*Finally. I have been small for long enough.*) and before ¶6, which becomes "Then Yuki is eleven pounds again…". She is still huge while they react. 40–70 words. The village is grave, not comic: to them a root-kin's great form after that sky is an omen or a rank they have only heard of; the humour comes from their seriousness, at most one deadpan James line. Old Wen may speak (formal, one or two sentences); don't repeat V1's moves (kneeling, praying, spears, "never in the history of the mountain", running to the Crimson Lotus) or V2's (the herb bag, counting claws, the joints). **Don't name Mei Lin** (she may not have met James yet). **No time words** (the scene can be day or night) and no "dust" (V1 just used it).
