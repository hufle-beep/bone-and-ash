# notes
- rr_hunt:4 assumes rainwater can pool in a split of the root's bark, and that Ya's spear has no sheath (the joke rests on it).
- rr_hunt:4: Ya's plan has James "nail its feet to the road" (bone darts through Convergence); cut if that reads as a skill named.
- hawks1:4 has Mei Lin quoting her mother on proper girls and game; an invented saying, in line with "raised on propriety".
- hawks1:5 (main) Ya's "I like watching it" is the flirt; the great form's "over everybody" assumes both women there, as the main version does.
- hawks1:5:away cat and human share "It was the rudest" (four words) on purpose; the great form differs. Change one if the checker counts across forms.
- Word counts taken with the {yuki} passage included: hawks1:4 about 85, hawks1:5 about 85, the away versions about 60 and 55.
