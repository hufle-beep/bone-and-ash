## 3. Considered and skipped

- **Already covered:** history of past lives with cause of death (问道 `lifespan.js`, last 20 lives) = group h #20/#21; a stat breakdown of every multiplier (仙途 §19) = Selene's; "a commitment says what it closes and what it brings" (Lowbridge) = our `col_warn` / `ns_warn`; a chore that warns it would overflow = Saturation stops at 95% (`breakAt`, `SIT_STOP_TEXT`); mastery that ends at a felt minimum = familiarity ×10, `sitMinutes` ≥ 1; seclusion as a time skip = `lf_seclusion` and "Cultivate until the limit"; Immortality Idle's carry-overs (round 1).
- **Real time and offline progress:** Dao Tree's seclusion cap as progression, 仙途's 8 h at 60%, 问道's 12 h and its 1 real day = 3 years of lifespan, Cultivation Legend's 48 h, hatching and building by real time, 仙途's deterministic real-time "天象": Bone and Ash has no real-time clock (owner's design since v0.11.64). The *framing* ("your seclusion deepens", what you keep, not what you lose) is noted for any later skip text.
- **Random rolls (no dice):** breakthrough failure with pity (仙途 +40% after failures, 问道 −8% tribulation damage per failure, 灵山掌门 success after three failures), fate and spirit-root rolls (仙途, 百世劫录, Dao Tree's roots), 8% spawns (Four Heavenly Valleys), shuffled hidden-outcome choices (仙途 §12). Our Intuitive Aptitude already eases the next try.
- **Lifespan as the clock:** Xianxia Simulator (80), Four Heavenly Valleys (120), 问道, 修仙家族模拟器: our clock is the act's deadline; lifespan goes in as flavour only (G1).

## 4. Avoid list (seen here)

- A hazard the player must answer with no tell, no result line, and help text that contradicts the rule (The Reincarnation System's demon bolts).
- A carry-over that silently stops working or can't be felt (The Reincarnation System's Insight at zero; Temporal Eden's soul system "does nothing" and softcaps that leave "very little progress between runs").
- A prestige that resets so much that each life is a regrind (Xiuzhen Idle; The Reincarnation System's first wheel), and the opposite: rebirth so cheap that the clock means nothing (Xiuzhen Idle's longevity). Ours: #53.
- Hidden or shifting gates: a breakthrough path players can't find (Four Heavenly Valleys), requirements that differ between runs (Xianxia Simulator), odds players dispute (修仙家族模拟器2); a stat that drains while resting (Xianxia Simulator's Mental State); a failed breakthrough that takes a stat away (Four Heavenly Valleys).
- Tooltips whose numbers disagree with the effect, and later bonuses that replace earlier ones without saying so (Infinite Dao's lifespan).
- Safety or automation settings that reset on restart (Infinite Dao).
- Too many attributes per person and too many pills and techniques to learn (修仙家族模拟器).
- Timed dailies, VIP tiers, gacha, battle passes (一念逍遥, 问道, `vue-idle-xiuxian`).
- Choices with no hint of what they do, in shuffled order, with random results (仙途 §12).
- Licence traps: a README badge saying MIT over a CC BY-NC licence file (`setube/vue-xiuxiangame`); "all rights reserved" forks of public-domain code (Lianara). Always trust the file.
