dp_hand | Ya takes the gristle out of your bowl with her fingers and eats it, not asking and not looking up. You turn the bowl so the rest is on her side. Mei Lin's mother watches the whole exchange and decides, visibly, that she saw nothing.
dp_spring_in | Mei Lin's mother asks whether the Blackroot offered anywhere decent to wash. "A hot spring," says Ya, in her careful supper voice, and then, because she was asked: "Room for two." The old woman's ladle stops over the pot, and goes on. Mei Lin studies her stew, and yours turns out to need your whole attention as well.
dp_spring_watch | Ya has leaned her spear in the corner behind your seat, not her own. It has been left in your reach before, at a hot spring, where you stood guard over the bathing with your back turned and steam on your neck. Mei Lin notes where it stands and says nothing, approvingly.
ec_mei_lin | To Mei Lin you are a stranger out of the trees, unlabelled and possibly a demon. You know which thumb will go to her hairpin's red tassel while she decides whether to believe you. It is the left. It goes there now, and you look politely at the grass.
ec_ya_meets | To Ya you are new: a tall thing that smells of nothing. Nothing about her is new to you. You know she stands with her weight on the back foot and the spear's butt against her heel, and that the tooth nearest her throat is the long one, with two small ones either side. You know which hand she eats with and which she keeps free. You keep the list to yourself.
fr_end_yuki | You do not turn to search Ashfall for a face. The one person you would search for is not down there. She is on this road with you, close enough to touch, and has already counted the crimson robes.
mei30 | Under the table, Mei Lin's foot comes to rest on top of yours and stays there. Above the table she is a dutiful daughter, asking her mother whether the well rope will see out the month. You keep your face in order, which is the one skill your education did give you, and leave your foot where it is. The rope will do, her mother rules. Mei Lin presses down once, lightly, for no reason she could give her mother, and goes on being a credit to the house.
ya45_camp | Ya picks up her sleeping hide, spear and all, puts it down hard against your bedroll, and sits on it to look at you. "I have thought about it, scholar," she says, loud enough for the whole camp. "I want you. I am telling you so that you are not surprised." You thank her for the warning. Over the flames Mei Lin observes that most people would put such a thing in a letter. Ya, who has never read one, says letters are for people who cannot find the man and quiet is for people who are not sure what they want, and goes on looking.
