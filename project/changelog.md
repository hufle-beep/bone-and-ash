| 0.11.117 | 2026-11-10 | (this push) | **The threat word.** A story card that leads to a fight now says how it would go: the game quietly plays that fight out with the blade alone (swing, then guard) from James's HP and Qi right now, and the card reads "fight · losing", "narrow", "costly" or "clean"; holding the card says the same in a sentence.
