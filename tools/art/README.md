# Pictures (art/pic) and how to remake them

v0.11.24 (owner picks A, R1, F1, K1, 2026-11-07). The game's art keys show a generated picture when `PICTURES` in `js/art/art.js` lists them; every other key keeps its code art until the last art push.

**Style:** the blended CLAMP (xxxHolic: elongated figures, sleepy almond eyes under heavy lids, hair in long locks, cloud curls, hatching) and Monogatari (flat two-tone, title-card look) prompt; the exact text is in `recipes.json`. Palette after the cut: black, white, greys, red; gold only for Yuki's eyes.

**Where they are made:** the owner's PC, Comfy Desktop (ComfyUI 0.44, port 8188), RTX 3080 10 GB, Z-Image-Turbo bf16 (`z_image_turbo_bf16`, `qwen_3_4b` as lumina2, `ae`), 8 steps, cfg 1, res_multistep / simple, ModelSamplingAuraFlow shift 3, 1280×800, about 10 s a picture. The originals (PNG, about 1.3 MB) stay in ComfyUI's output folder under `boneash/`, with a zip backup there.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `recipes.json` | Every picture: its steps in order (generate, inpaint, detail inpaint, local edits), prompts, seeds, strengths, masks, the rename and the palette step |
| `workflow.json` | The three ComfyUI API graphs (`generate`, `inpaint`, `detail_inpaint` = crop, scale up, repaint, scale down, paste) |
| `masks.py` | Draws the masks of `recipes.json` as PNGs to upload |
| `palette.py` | Cuts a picture to the palette at 800×500 WebP (`--gold` keeps gold, Yuki only) |
| `ouroboros.py` | Inks James's ouroboros (a ring biting its tail) onto the left forearm |
| `palette_check.py` | The full check's palette step: fails on colours outside the palette, or gold outside Yuki's pictures |

**Remaking or changing a picture:** run its steps from `recipes.json` in ComfyUI (the same seed gives the same picture), then `python3 -I tools/art/palette.py OUT [--gold] LAST.png`, rename to the `art/pic` name, run the full check. A new picture: add its steps here, its key to `PICTURES`, its file to `sw.js` FILES.

**Rules learnt (2026-11-07):** Z-Image-Turbo ignores negative prompts; say everything in the prompt. Don't ask it for "black and white only" in plain anime prompts (posterized results); the style text above works. A leftover reference image in NanoGPT gave pixelated pictures. Small parts (eyes, tattoos) repaint far better as a detail inpaint (crop scaled up 3–4×). The Blood Ravens emblem (Games Workshop) is not drawn; James's raven is his own.

**v0.11.38 (P-b; made as the v0.11.26 work in progress), the prologue (42 pictures):** every key of the prologue's part of the art table has a picture named after the key. How they were made: 3 seeds per key (22 / 44 / 55; redos 66 / 77 / 88 / 99) from one generate step each, a contact sheet per batch cut to the palette, the owner's pick. Each prompt is a style text from `recipes.json` `styles` (**p** for scenes with people, **n** for animals, objects and landscapes only) plus the scene, with the characters described word for word from `recipes.json` `cast` (James, Yuki the cat, Mei Lin, Gao Feng, Lu Han, Old Wen, Mei Lin's mother, the villagers, the Sect men, Pock-Face). `jackals_dead` adds an inpaint (mask `jd_jackals`) that repaints the two jackals dead. Queueing from the Claude app's browser: POST the `generate` graph of `workflow.json` to `http://127.0.0.1:8188/prompt` with the prompt, seed and `boneash/<file>` filled in; an inpaint first uploads its source and mask to `/upload/image` (subfolder `boneash`, type `input`).

**Rules learnt (the prologue pictures):** in busy scenes the model adds stray tall women in black dresses and giant floating faces (the "title card" habit). Writing "no women" or "no faces" makes it worse; once an animals-only prompt with "no people. no women" came back with nude figures (deleted unseen). What works: describe what fills the background ("the background is a wall of tree trunks and ferns", "the sky is empty and pale"), use the **n** style when James is not in the scene, and check every seed before it is shown. A bowl in the forest floor is drawn as a cooking bowl: say "a sunken hollow". Yuki's eyes go grey in scene pictures (gold is kept only in her own pictures).

**v0.11.49 (Y3 part 1, the great cat; owner picks 2026-11-07 20:33–22:55):** each of the 29 keys in `YUKI_PICS` has `<file>_tiger.webp`, shown while Yuki is the great cat (`YUKI_PIC_FORMS` in `js/art/art.js`). Fresh pictures reuse the key's prompt with the cat line swapped for cast `yuki_tiger` (redos `yuki_tiger2`, a clearer tiger); `james` and `jackals_dead` are inpaints on the approved pictures; `ridge` became a new landscape with no characters (drawn from a description, never traced from a found photo); stray figures and faces are inpainted out with a prompt that describes only what belongs there. Scenes are cut without `--gold`. Running a session from the Claude app: the job lists go to ComfyUI's `input/boneash_jobs/` and are read by the page at `http://127.0.0.1:8188` (`/view?…&type=input`), sources and masks to `input/boneash/`; a step on an earlier output can read it as `boneash/<file>_00001_.png [output]` once it exists.

**Rules learnt (Y3):** negations still fail ("nothing on his shoulder" drew a cat there; describe the shoulder instead); a detail inpaint at 3× on a face looks too realistic beside the flat style (a 1× inpaint with "flat two-tone ink, no realistic shading" fits); the style text's cloud curls leak into small repaints; a narrow mask cuts the animal (give it room); head repaints from text alone do not make James look the same across pictures; that needs a reference tool (the owner's next art step). ComfyUI does not learn between runs, so a restart does not fix a repeated mistake.
